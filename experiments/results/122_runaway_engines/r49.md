## What changed

- **Holding limits.** A player holds at most **150 credits, 10 energy, 10 chips and 10 ambassadors** in supply
  (`economy.hold_credits`, `hold_energy`, `hold_chips`, `hold_ambassadors`). A gain past a limit is lost
  (`economy.hold_lost.<resource>`), setup's and a threat stake coming back included. With no room at all an offer
  whose only gain it is isn't made: a card or power whose every part is a capped gain (r48's nothing-to-act-on rule),
  the gain-an-ambassador project, selling a chip or a card at the credit limit, Withdraw, buying a city's energy.
  One home: `Cx::add_resource` clamps every gain; `World::room` answers every offer.
- **The credit loop's cap** (after Matt's message 5134). Credit production raised by cards (programs, Copy) stops
  at **60** (`economy.credit_production`); a raise past it is lost (`economy.production_lost.credits`). One home:
  `economy::raise_production`.
- **No card changed.** Patience stays uncapped (Matt, message 5117).
