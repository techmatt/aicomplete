# 173: the cold readers' questions, answered against the engine

Every question a cold reader asked, its answer from each draft, and the engine's answer (evidence: file:line,
checked in the code by a verifier who did not write the rulebook). Raw outputs, paragraph notes included, are in
[raw/](raw/). `first` is the first draft (172), read by G (general hobby gamer) and V (Spirit Island veteran);
`second` is the second draft before its last fixes, read by fresh G2 and V2, who answered all 50 plus 10 of their own.

## The 50 review questions

| id | question | engine | first | second (G2 / V2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | What does the Protector start with? | 1 node on the starting hex. It is the economy track's first token, so the track counter is set to 1 and the space's +1 insight income applies from round 1. Also 1 ambassador in supply, 3 energy, 46 credits, and the Protector's cube on the nearest clean city of its country (tie: lower hex). No cards at setup. | right | right / right |
| G2 | Does my setup node cost energy? | No. Setup puts the node on the hex and sets the track counter to 1 without charging anything, and the first economy space's printed cost is 0 anyway. | right | right / right |
| G3 | At difficulty 0 with the Militarist, how many agents are in each country's pool at setup? | 1 agent per country. Pool = ambassador_income[d0] = 0, plus Garrisons' increase_income +1 = 1. fill_pools puts one round of that into each country's pool at setup. | right | right / right |
| G4 | In a 2-player game, which boards are used? | Builder (seat 0) and Protector (seat 1). Seat s takes ai.boards[s mod 3], so there is no Researcher. | right | right / right |
| G5 | In round 1, do we get cards before the adversary acts? | No. Round order is sway, adversary event, threats deal, build (Rally/Deploy/projects/botnets), corrupt, natural, march, reveal+income, and only then cards.tier/deal/draft/keep. Round 1 is no different. | right | right / right |
| G6 | Can I save unspent insight for next round? | No. At round start, insight is reset to income + gifts + carried - debt. Only a board with the Patience passive (the Researcher) carries over, and only its own unspent insight: what it holds minus insight received from other seats that round. | right | right / right |
| G7 | After I pass, can I still chip in on a threat if a teammate asks? | No. A passed seat gets no legal actions for the rest of the combined action phase (cards.play+threats.counter+...), and a threat payment is one of those actions. The flag resets only when the next decision phase begins. | right | right / right |
| G8 | Does a factory a city builds this production make chips this round? | No. In the production 'bases' step the order is measure, extra corp troops, factory_output, then grow_factories. A factory built by a city this production produces from next round. | right | right / right |
| G9 | When do agents added by agent income actually land? | Income goes into each country's pool in the adversary's reveal step (step 12 in the rulebook), after the march. The pool lands in full at the next round's Rally, in the build step, and the Rally then empties it. | right | right / right |
| G10 | Can I place a node on a corrupted city or a hex with agents? | Yes. The node must go on a non-ocean hex with no node of any player, no corruption marker and no land corruption, next to one of your nodes. With no nodes left, any such hex in your home country. Agents and a corrupted site are not checked. | right | right / right |
| G11 | Can I Move an ambassador onto an empty open hex? | No. Move targets only hexes in reach that hold a city, a base, a station or factory, or any player's node, and never the hex your only ambassador already stands on. | right | right / right |
| G12 | If I liberate a corrupted city, is it mine? | Not by the Liberate action. The agents on the city cancel, the adversary cube comes off, Unity sways and the city is clean (neutral), with free_leaves 0 agents landing. Making it yours takes a Claim: claim_city 4 plus 1 per agent, in reach, no botnet on or next to it, and no Embargo. The exception is the tier-2 card Hostile Buyout (align: true, in the baseline deck), which puts your cube on the city it liberates. | right | right / right |
| G13 | How many ambassadors to claim a neutral station with 2 agents on it? | 5 from your supply: claim_station 3 plus 2 agents. The agents are removed (cancel) and your cube goes on. Ambassadors standing on the site are not counted (claim_cost subtracts nothing) and stay where they are. | right | right / right |
| G14 | When I liberate, are a teammate's ambassadors standing on the site spent? | Yes. A Liberate spends the ambassadors standing on the site first, the acting player's and then every other player's in seat order, automatically; the supply pays the rest. The engine never asks the teammate. | right | right / right |
| G15 | Can I pay toward a threat if only my ambassador (no node) is next to its token? | Yes. You are near a threat if one of your nodes or one of your ambassadors is on or next to any of its tokens. | right | right / right |
| G16 | Can we split a threat's payment across its two options? | No. The threat is countered only when the pooled payments cover every item of one resource option. Payments are pooled by resource, so money put toward one option never adds to the other one. | right | right / right |
| G17 | Can two players combine sacrificed cards for a tag counter? | No. One near player alone sacrifices cards in play (programs, or building stacks) carrying the tags. The board's own tag and track tags are not sources. | right | right / right |
| G18 | If nobody is near an unpaid threat, who takes the harm? | The player whose node is the fewest hexes from any of its tokens, at any distance, whether or not anyone is near. On a tie the tied players pick one. A player with no node on the board can't be hit. | right | partly / partly |
| G19 | Can a threat destroy my last node in stage III? | Yes, in one case. A 'destroy a node' harm never takes your last node, and neither does Siege's node loss. But Overrun (tier 2) corrupts the uncorrupted city its token is on, and corruption destroys any node on that hex. Only the stage-I last-node rule guards that, so from stage II on it can take your last node, unless Shelter, hardening, your ambassador, Aegis or Sanctuary saves it. Casus Belli's extra war step next round can also take a last node on a factory that war destroys. | wrong | right / right |
| G20 | At 0-2 Unity tips, do agents sitting in pools stop the purge? | No. The purge counts only pieces on board hexes: agents (and botnets, troops, corrupted cities) on hexes. Agents waiting in a country's pool are a counter, not a piece on the board, and don't count. | right | right / right |
| G21 | An adversary troop is deployed on my factory. Does it hit it in the march? | Usually yes. Deploy lands on the lowest-numbered factory of any owner, skipping a hex with the Protector's node. In the same turn's march the troop's first step strikes a target on or next to its hex; if there are several, the players choose which, so it may hit a neighbor instead. Stoppers in order: Shelter, a corp troop at a base on or next to the hex (it is removed), a hardening token, the factory owner's ambassador, Aegis. The troop is used up either way. Shelter can't actually be up then: it is set in the action phase and falls at cleanup. If corruption earlier in the turn took the factory, it is no longer a target. | partly | partly / partly |
| G22 | In stage II, war hits a factory with my node on it. Can my ambassador there save the node? | Only if the factory is yours. The factory owner's ambassador on the hex stops the war strike, so the factory and your node both survive. If the factory is neutral or another player's, your ambassador does nothing, and if the factory goes, the node goes with it (from stage II, even your last). | partly | right / right |
| G23 | Can I use Unity swayed this action to remove Discord if Discord would still be at or above its tip point? | No. Hold-off amounts are offered only if they leave the Discord track below its tip point (or 0). Otherwise all of the swayed Unity goes onto the Unity track. | right | right / right |
| G24 | How many Discord tips can we survive? | The Discord deck holds 6 cards and never reshuffles, so the 7th tip loses. At difficulty 4 one card is cut (5 left, and the tip count starts at 1), so the 6th tip loses. | right | right / right |
| G25 | Can Shelter protect my node from the adversary's Corrupt step? | No, in effect. Corruption's node destruction does check Shelter, but Shelter can only be used in the action phase (by power or card) and is cleared at cleanup, so it is never up during the adversary's turn. It does cover threats (after production) and war. Sanctuary, hardening, your ambassador and Aegis can still save the node. | right | right / right |
| V1 | Do any agents or troops start on the board at setup, like Spirit Island's initial explorers and towns? | No agents or troops are on the board at setup. Each country gets an adversary cube on its marked city, an adversary cube and a botnet on its marked station, a neutral station and two neutral factories. Each country's agent pool starts with one round of agent income: ambassador_income[0] = 0 at difficulty 0, plus Garrisons' +1, so 1 agent. That agent lands at round 1's Rally (adversary.build). Bases start with no corp troops. Adversary troops first arrive with a Deploy line or the Protector-troop step. | right | right / right |
| V2 | How much insight do I have in round 1? | 6. At round start your insight is set to the board's 5 plus the income of your uncovered track spaces. The setup node counts as the first economy-track token, and that space prints +1 insight income each round. Round 1's opening tier-2 keeps (1 insight each) then come out of these 6. | right | right / right |
| V3 | In a 2-player game, which boards are used? | Builder (seat 0) and Protector (seat 1). Seat s takes boards[s mod 3] from [Builder, Protector, Researcher], so no Researcher plays with 2. | right | right / right |
| V4 | Do we get cards before the adversary's first turn? | No. The round order is sway, then the adversary's event, threat deal, build (Rally/Deploy), corrupt, natural corruption, march and reveal, then cards.deal (round 1: the opening hand), keep, then the action phase. | right | right / right |
| V5 | Can I use this round's upkeep income to pay a threat before it fires? | No. threats.counter is offered only inside the action-phase decision. board.produce and economy.upkeep run after it, then threats.execute. Upkeep income arrives too late for this round's threats (a delayed threat can use it next round). | right | right / right |
| V6 | Spirit Island habit: can I hold a 'slow' card until after the adversary acts? | Correct. There is no fast/slow timing. The only play window is the action phase, which always comes after that round's adversary turn. A held card can next be played after the next round's adversary turn. Hands carry over (no hand limit). | right | right / right |
| V7 | After I pass, can I still sell a card or add to a threat? | No. Once a seat passes, it gets no legal actions until the next decision phase. Selling, paying a threat and taking back contributions all stop. Passed flags reset only when a new decision phase begins. | right | right / right |
| V8 | Does a Shelter used this action phase protect against corruption in next round's adversary turn? | Shelter sets the country's Shelter flag to 1 for the rest of the round. Cleanup counts it down to 0, so it never protects in next round's adversary turn. While it is up, destroy_piece refuses every destruction in that country, war included: a war strike on a factory in a sheltered country fails (the troop is spent), so a node on that factory survives too. The war rule that 'no Shelter saves that node' only matters when the factory is actually destroyed, and that can't happen in a sheltered country. Only the stage-I last-node check runs inside lose_node. | partly | right / right |
| V9 | Can insight I gain during the adversary turn (Vigil) be spent this round? | Yes. Round start sets insight first. Vigil's 'gain 1 insight' fires during board.march, in the adversary turn, and adds straight to the resource, so it can be spent in this round's action phase. The next round start resets it, and the Protector has no Patience, so it is lost then. | right | right / right |
| V10 | Can I place a node on a hex with agents, or on a corrupted city? | Yes to both. node_sites allows any non-ocean hex adjacent to your node that has no node of anyone's, no corruption marker and no land corruption. Agents, botnets and adversary cubes don't block a placement. | right | right / right |
| V11 | Spirit Island habit: can my node share a hex with another player's node, or can I stack two of mine? | No to both. A hex holding any player's node is excluded (node_owner(g,h).is_none()), so a hex holds at most one node. | right | right / right |
| V12 | My ambassador already stands on a neutral factory. Does it count toward the 3 for Claim? | No. Claim costs claim_N + the agents on the site, all paid from the acting seat's supply. Ambassadors standing on the site are ignored. Liberate spends the ambassadors standing on the site first (the actor's, then the others' in seat order) and the supply pays the rest. | right | right / right |
| V13 | Does a botnet on a corrupted station stop me liberating it? Is the station then mine? | Correct. liberable checks only the price and reach, never botnets. liberate_site takes off the adversary cube and the agents and sways 2 Unity; the botnet stays, and the station turns neutral, not yours. can_claim refuses while a botnet is on or next to the hex (next_to includes the hex itself), so you can't then Claim it. | right | right / right |
| V14 | Can I Move an ambassador onto an open hex next to my node that has no site, base or node? | No. Move targets only a hex in reach that holds a city, a base, a station or factory, or any node (has_piece). An empty open hex is not offered. Remove agents can go onto any hex in reach that has at least one agent. | right | right / right |
| V15 | Who takes the harm from an unpaid threat? | The player whose node is the fewest hexes from any of its tokens, wherever that node is. Only nodes count, not ambassadors. On a tie the tied players pick one (in the engine the lowest tied seat answers). A player with no node can't be hit. | right | partly / partly |
| V16 | Can a player with no node or ambassador near a threat chip in credits? | No. Paying (and the tag counter) needs the seat to be near: a node or ambassador of theirs on or next to one of its tokens. A player no longer near may still take back what they paid. Card counters, jams and delays also reach only threats you are near; Unity rewards and standing counters reach any threat. | right | right / right |
| V17 | At stage III, can a threat destroy my last node? | Partly yes. A 'destroy a node' harm (Dark Cities, Purge Protocols, Grid Collapse, Overload, Last Ultimatum) and Siege's factory destruction never take a last node, at any stage. But Overrun (corrupt the city here) and Station Seizure (corrupt the station here) corrupt the hex through the normal corruption code, which destroys a node on it. That is subject only to the stage-I last-node rule, so from stage II on they can destroy a last node. Shelter, hardening, the node owner's ambassador, Aegis Doctrine or Sanctuary can still stop it. | wrong | right / right |
| V18 | We put 4 credits on a 6-credit threat and couldn't finish. Do we lose the 4? | No. At execution, any threat now fully paid is countered. Every other threat's payments go back to their payers before any threat acts, a waiting threat's included. | right | right / right |
| V19 | At difficulty 1, does a '2 Security tags' counter become 3? | No. The difficulty steps add only to resource items (Pay). A tag-sacrifice item is passed through unchanged, so '2 Security tags' stays 2 (and 3 for tier 2) at every difficulty. | wrong | right / right |
| V20 | At 0-2 Unity tips, do agents waiting in the pools stop the purge? | No. Pools are per-country counters (adversary.pool.N), not pieces on a hex. The purge counts only hex tokens: corrupted cities, botnets, agents and troops on hexes. | right | right / right |
| V21 | At 6 Unity tips, a corrupted station and land corruption remain but no corrupted cities. Do we win? | Yes. At 6 Unity tips (purge_at [3,6,6] -> 3 steps -> CitiesOnly), only corrupted cities count. You win at the end-of-action check that follows the action or step, not mid-action. | right | right / right |
| V22 | Spirit Island habit: can an adversary troop attack my node or my aligned city? | No. An adversary troop strikes only player-owned, uncorrupted research stations and factories. Its node-strike switch, board.troop_node, is 4, a stage that never comes, so nodes are never targets. Cities are never targets. | right | right / right |
| V23 | In a stage-II round, can a Corrupt line destroy my last node and lose us the game? | Yes. Corruption destroys the node on the hex through destroy_piece. The last-node rule holds only while the stage is below 2. From stage II on, a last node goes unless one of these stops it: Shelter on the country, a hardening token on the hex, the node owner's ambassador on the hex (the site owner's cube is already gone), Aegis Doctrine, or Sanctuary. A player with no nodes then loses the game for everyone at the end-of-step check. | partly | right / right |
| V24 | If we spend swayed Unity to hold Discord off, does it also count toward the Unity tip? | No. Of the Unity waiting at the end of the action, the k used to hold Discord off moves Discord off the track. Only n - k goes onto the Unity track. Only amounts that leave the Discord track below its tipping point are offered. | right | right / right |
| V25 | When Discord tips with 2 over, does the overflow carry over? When do we lose to Discord? | No carry for Discord: a Discord tip removes the whole track (carry 1 keeps overflow for Unity only). With 6 Discord cards, the 7th Discord tip finds the deck empty and the players lose. At difficulty 4, one card is cut and the tip count starts at 1, so the 6th real tip loses. | right | right / right |

| N7 | Corrupt land hits an open hex with my node: can I rebuild there later? | No: land corruption goes on the hex (even if the node is protected and survives), no node can be placed on land corruption, nothing removes it, and it sways 1 Discord a round. | N8 | A corrupted factory makes a troop in production: when does it act? | The troop is placed on the factory's hex in production step 2 and acts in the next adversary turn's march (step 11), taking 2 steps (strike a target on or next to it, else move toward the nearest). Until then it counts against the purge at 0-5 Unity tips. | right |
| N9 | Does Shelter stop war destroying a neutral factory, which would add Discord, in that country? | Yes: Shelter in the factory's country refuses every destruction of any owner's piece, war included; the factory survives and war adds Discord only when a factory is actually destroyed. | right |
| N10 | A Discord tip in production (from city Discord) draws a card that deals a threat: when can we pay it? | It waits: a threat dealt after the counter phase skips this round's execution, can be paid in next round's action phase, and executes in next round's threat step with that round's threats. | right |

## Final draft: G3 on the retest's trouble spots

| id | question | engine | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Nobody is near an unpaid threat. Two players' nodes are tied for closest to its token. Who takes the harm? | The harm hits the seat whose node is closest to any token, at any distance (near or not). On a tie, the lowest tied seat makes the choice among the tied seats (choose_loss). | right |
| F2 | An adversary troop is deployed on my factory. Does it hit that factory in this turn's march? | Yes. Deploy (adversary.build) comes before board.march in the same turn. The march lists every troop, the deployed one included (no standing flag on event Deploy lines). On step 1 the troop has a target on or next to its hex, so it strikes; the players choose if there are several targets. Protection: Shelter (cleared at cleanup, so it is never up in the adversary turn), then a corp troop at a base within 1, then hardening, then the owner's ambassador, then Aegis. The troop is spent either way. A walled hex can't be a landing spot. | right |
| F3 | What is a coastal city? | A city with an ocean hex next to it, or a city the piece prints as coastal. | right |
| F4 | Can a new corruption spread to a neighboring hex by itself? Which things can corrupt a site? | No corruption spreads by itself. A site corrupts through natural corruption, through any Corrupt X line (on the event card, but also on Discord cards Blight, Offensive and Firestorm, and on projects Infiltration Drive and Grand Offensive), through threats (Overrun, Station Seizure, Factory Occupation), or through the project Station Coup, which flips stations. | partly |
| F5 | Does a corrupted factory make a troop every production? Can I stop it? | Yes, in production each corrupted factory with no player ambassador on it places 1 adversary troop on its hex. The Wall does not stop it. A player ambassador blocks it, and liberating the factory makes it neutral, so it makes nothing. An occupied factory also makes nothing. | right |
| F6 | In stage II, war strikes a neutral factory with my node and my ambassador on it. Is my node saved? | No. The war strike calls destroy_piece on the FACTORY. Armor comes only from the cube owner's ambassador, and a neutral factory has no owner, so there is no armor. When the factory is destroyed, lose_node takes the node (only a stage-I last node is safe). Shelter, a hardening token on the hex (for example a shield on that node hex) or Aegis would save the factory and so the node. | right |
| F7 | Same, but the factory is mine. Is my node saved? | Yes. The cube owner's ambassador on the hex absorbs the strike and is spent, so the factory survives and the node stays. Shelter or a hardening token is checked first and would spare the ambassador. | right |
| F8 | I claim a neutral factory where my own ambassador already stands. Does it help pay? What happens to it? | No. The claim costs claim_factory 3 + 1 per agent, paid from the supply (target_price). claim_site removes only agents and adds the cube, so the standing ambassador stays on what is now the player's own site. | right |
| F9 | At level 4, how many Discord tips can we survive, and at what tier is the first Discord card resolved? | The level-4 deck has 5 Discord cards and the tip count starts at 1. The first tip is tip 2, which resolves at tier I (tier_at 3,6). The team survives 5 tips and loses on the 6th. | right |
| F10 | The Deploy line's lowest-numbered factory holds the Protector's node. Where does the troop land? | Walled hexes are filtered out first. The troop goes to the lowest-numbered factory in the country without the Protector's node (any owner's). With none, it goes to the lowest-numbered such city. With none, it does not land. | right |
| F11 | Every neutral research station in a country is firewalled and the card says Rally stations. Where do the agents go? | Firewalled sites are excluded. Without them the neutral class is empty, so the agents go to player-owned stations; failing those, to the adversary's. With no site at all the pool is zeroed and nothing is placed, so the agents stay in the supply. | right |
| F12 | Does an event card ever have no Rally? What happens to the pool at a Rally? | Every event card's standing lines include a Rally. The whole pool is zeroed and spread evenly (n/k each), and the players choose which sites take the remainder, one each. With nowhere to land, the agents stay in the supply. An arrival cancels with a player ambassador. Under a cordon the pool is emptied and nothing lands. | right |
| F13 | Can a threat destroy my last node in stage III? | A threat that corrupts a site (Overrun, Station Seizure, Factory Occupation) goes through corrupt_hex -> destroy_piece, where the last node is safe only below stage II, so it can destroy a last node in stage III. DestroyNode/EachDestroyNode from threats and Siege's destroy_facility never take a last node. | right |
| F14 | At level 1, does a threat's "2 Security tags" item cost more? | No. Surcharges apply only to CounterItem::Pay items; tag items pass through unchanged. | right |
| F15 | Does a Shelter played this round protect against next round's corruption? | No. The Shelter flag is set to 1 and counted down at cleanup, so it is gone by the next adversary turn. Within its round it stops destruction (of a node too). A site still corrupts under it; Shelter only saves what would be destroyed. | right |
| F16 | Can land corruption be removed? | No. LAND_CORRUPTION is only ever added (corrupt_hex). It drips 1 Discord each round in sway and blocks node placement. No code removes it. | right |
| F17 | I used Remove agents on a hex with 5 agents. What is left on the hex, and what happens when another agent lands there? | remove_count 3 agents are removed and 1 ambassador is added with no cancel, leaving the ambassador plus 2 agents. A later arriving agent cancels with the ambassador (both removed), and the 2 agents remain. | right |
