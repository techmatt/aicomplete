## What changed

- **Energy as a second cost** on five power-hungry buildings, with credits cut about 4.5 an energy so pay holds at the
  anchors: Fabrication Megaplex 25 cr + 2 energy (was 34), Lights-Out Assembly 10 + 1 (14), Orbital Mass Driver 15 + 3
  (28), Foundation Model Campus 13 + 2 (22), Positron Storage Ring 9 + 2 (18). Industry and Power are the usual
  consumers; AI compute contributes one card.
- **Late sinks:** Particle Accelerator (tier 1, 16 cr, 1 ins: "Action: pay 2 energy, 3 research cubes on a megaproject")
  and Citywide Microgrid (tier 2, 4 cr + 8 energy, 2 ins: liberate a corrupted city in reach; "Action: 2 Sway toward
  Unity"). The deck is now 151 cards.
- **Moonshot Grant stays at 46** (Matt, message 5254): it is bound by offers, not by price.
- **D51:** Total War's face now says its ambassadors land one a base. **D48:** the three icons were already there
  (checked in the viewer).
- **Measures, no play change:** `games.jsonl` adds `spent` and `uses` (what a seat spent, and on what). New counters:
  `economy.hold_lost_seat.<seat>.<r>`, `economy.hold_lost.<r>.rNN` (124's empty by-round read now fills),
  `economy.hold_lost_phase.<i>.<r>`, `board.completion_lost.energy`, `energy_cards.held|short.<seat>`.
  `apply_overlay.py` can now write a whole new card row.

## The Protector and the Researcher

They don't chase energy. Fresh, the Protector gained 21.1 → 19.0 a seat-game and the Researcher 11.8 → 12.9; peaks are
6.5-6.9 against r49's 6.6-6.7. They play more energy-cost cards, because more cards cost energy: the Protector 0.20 →
0.49 a seat-game, the Researcher 0.18 → 0.68 (the Builder 0.38 → 0.65). One sits in hand short of energy more often.
The share of seat-games where an energy-cost card was in hand, at the end of some action phase, needing more energy
than the seat held: Protector 29% → 70%, Researcher 25% → 61% (Builder 22% → 56%). That counts a card kept for a later
round, and energy then is mostly spent on nodes. Lights-Out Assembly, Cryptojacking Campaign (unchanged), Fabrication
Megaplex and Citywide Microgrid are the cards most often held short.
