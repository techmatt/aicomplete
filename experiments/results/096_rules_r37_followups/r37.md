# 096: rules r37, follow-ups from 093

Rules revision 37 (bumped from the constant, 36 to 37). Every rule here is a candidate.

## What changed

- **Converters** need the player's node on a city of the megaproject's country (any city, owned or
  not), where r36 took any node there (`board::converter_blocked`, `common::has_city_node_in`; the
  block reads "no node in a city here"). Starting nodes are off the cities, so a seat qualifies only
  once it places a node on one.
- **Popular Uprising** is one Action: it arrives with 6 Followers; *Action: pay 6 Followers, liberate
  a corrupted city in reach, then discard it.* 36 credits (from 19), 1 insight. It fits the top box at
  7.5 pt (`npm run cards:fit`: 0 of 159 cards below).
- **Continental Supergrid** 22 credits (from 6), 2 insight.
- **Dark cities (amendment #4040):** an adversary ambassador on a neutral city stays and the city
  sells no energy while it stands there (`board.city_dark` counts the city-rounds). On an aligned
  city the ambassador still diverts that round's energy and goes.
- **`tips`** is gone from the `Ability` schema (no card printed it; the face and `branch_blocked`
  read it).
- **Reach:** the exceptions' reasons are written into rules.md (*Terms, Reach*; §3 Spread; §4's war
  node loss; §8 *Moving ambassadors*). No distance changed.

## Agents

- `w.converter_city` 2: a seat holding cubes with a node in a city of a completed megaproject's country.
- `w.city_dark` 4: a neutral city in some seat's reach with an adversary ambassador on it. Before it,
  the eval saw only the corruption pressure of an ambassador on a city, not the lost sale.
- `CardValue` (value.rs) and its mirror in `effect_ledger.py`: a one-shot Action with no OR branch is
  used once, not `horizon x uses_share` times, and the tokens the card arrives with are its fuel, not
  value. Without this the new Popular Uprising read 94 credits to the agents.

Findings:

- Converter uses fall by half (d2 2.18 → 1.11, d0 2.51 → 1.42): the gate bites as calibrated.
  `converter_city` doesn't move them (1.11 on, 1.10 off) or the win rate: it is kept as a small,
  null term.
- Popular Uprising liberates in 90% of the games it is played (from 57%), at the same play rate.
- Continental Supergrid was all but unplayed at 6 (0.01 a game) and is unplayed at 22: its
  6-Power gate is what holds it back, not its price.
- Dark cities: about 5.8 city-rounds a game. Energy bought falls a
  quarter (3.5 → 2.6); **nodes placed do not fall** (17.0 → 16.9 d2, 18.2 → 18.2 d0), so seats don't
  starve. **Corruptions rise by about one a game** (d2 1.6 → 2.6, d0 1.0 → 1.9): before r37 each
  production stripped a neutral city's ambassador, which slowed every city's climb to `corrupt_at`.
  That is the gradient Matt named (one darkens, four corrupt). `city_dark` earns +6.9 ± 3.8 at d2.

Calibration, reasoned beside the first read (not before it): an ambassador reaches a neutral city
by arrivals (adversary cards land them on cities and pieces) and by spread, which sends extras to
pieces and lets clean cities collect any number. At r36 production removed it each round, so a
neutral city was dark only the round one arrived; at r37 it stays until a player evicts it or
`corrupt_at` corrupts the city, so dark rounds are the r36 arrival rate times how long ambassadors
stand. The read (5.8 a game) says that is several rounds each.
