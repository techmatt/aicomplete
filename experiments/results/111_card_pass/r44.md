# 111: the card pass, r44

Matt's rulings of 2026-11-04 (five in the prompt, two by amendment 4674) and his unit anchors, applied to every
card; the loop bug fixed at its cause; a ballpark read of r44 against r43. The card list, every changed, new and cut
card with its reason and anchor check: [cards.md](cards.md). The read's script: [credits_read.py](credits_read.py)
(`python experiments/results/111_card_pass/credits_read.py OLD_RUN NEW_RUN`).

## How the pass was made

Six proposers (one per tag section) priced every card at the anchors and proposed keep, change or cut; three
reviewers (a player's lens, the anchor arithmetic, the card rules) read the merged deck; I consolidated, applied it,
read r44 once, and two more reviewers read the final deck as printed. Both review rounds' flags were answered card by
card in cards.md (the "(final review)" lines are the second round's).

## Engine changes (each needed by a ruling or the loop)

- **The loop** (109: 2061 plays at r43; a second form at r44's first read, 2308 plays of Credential Harvest). Two
  causes, both fixed: an event went to the discard *before* it resolved, so a draw on an empty deck reshuffled it
  back into the hand (now `cards.resolving.N` holds it while it resolves); and a draw reshuffled the discards
  mid-round, so two draw events (Breakthrough Leak, Capital Injection) drew each other back forever. **A draw now
  never reshuffles**: only the draft does, so a card played this round can't return this round. Engine test
  `a_draw_event_cannot_draw_itself_back`. After the fix the most plays of one card in a game is 6 (Hardpoint
  Lockdown, across rounds); no game times out.
- **Removal in reach** (ruling 21): `reach` on `remove_ambassadors_country` and `remove_troops` (any hexes within
  reach, across countries; the face prints "in reach"). The agents value it at the same rows.
- **Feeders never veto** (ruling 20): `add_tokens` onto one of your cards and a `cash_out` from one are in
  feasibility's "only veto a card that does nothing" group, as draws and gifts are; before, a feeder with no target
  building could not be played at all, whatever its main effect.
- **"Another player" means you in a 1-player game** (ruling 25, amendment 4674): a gift lands on you; a teammate
  trigger fires on your own plays, a shared gain landing once.

## Gifts and loops with the team as one pool (ruling 25)

- Circular under the pool, fixed: Shared Grid Accord (pay 2 energy, another gains 4: now gives 2, costs no energy),
  Mutual Aid Pact (pay 10 credits, another gains 8 credits: the gift is 3 insight), Vendor Financing (a credit gift:
  2 chips).
- Teammate triggers (Substation Hub, Consulate Tower, Joint Task Force) fire once a card played: no loop; capped by
  cards. In a 1-player game they fire on your own plays, so Joint Task Force reads about 2.5 there (1.4 at two seats).
- The help cards are one-shot programs; no gift Action is left in the deck (the r36 gift buildings are gone).
- Repeatable loops found and capped: the drain standard action (5 credits, no limit a round) fired Intrusion Lab and
  Security Ops Floor for more than it cost (cap: compute on the projects; payoffs cut to 1 antimatter, 1 insight); the
  Remove standard action and Checkpoint Network fired Honeypot Relay for more than the Action cost (cap: ambassadors
  in reach; 3 credits a fire); Agency Directorate's "at 3, draw 3" refunded a card a tag played (cap: the tier-1 deck,
  which no longer reshuffles; now at 4). Exchanges (ruling 24): no card converts at a free ratio; Collateralized
  Grid's one-shot cash-out is one use and stays.

## The read (r44 against r43, paired, seeds 33836-33883, 48 a cell, d2)

The table is [read.md](read.md), on the final build. Strategist +19 / +6 / -2 (bp / br / pr), greedy +0 / +12 / -2,
each ±7-12: bp's strategist the only cell past 2 SE. Rounds unchanged (6.6 strategist, 4.9-5.3 greedy). **Credits
left at game end per seat** did not move: strategist 49 a seat (r43 47; cells 39-63 against 32-66), greedy 14 (r43
16). The throughput and the dearer late cards did not shrink the pile at these agents.

Plays (the strategist, a game): the feeders went from 0.03-0.09 to 0.5-0.84; every new card is played (0.09-0.75);
Forensic Audit (0.03), Great Liberation (0.01) and Kinetic Interdiction (0.10) stay rare. Checkpoint Network at 4
credits an Action was never kept (the agents read 2 removals at 4.4); at 2 it is kept in 16% of games.

**Agent cost:** per decision the strategist is about 1.6× r43 on the read (4.65 ms against 2.99), greedy 1.6× (3.3
against 2.0, a noisier cell); `per_decision.py` on 8 games read the strategist at 3.6×. Hands are bigger (37% more
cards drawn a game, 15-18% more options a decision), and a profile shows the usual lookahead / settled / eval shape,
no new hot spot. Kept as the cost of Matt's throughput lever; `speed.py` gives no verdict across the revision, and its
baseline was not moved: the next agents item should read it.
