# 108: engines worth playing (r43)

r42's engines lost to the cards they displaced (107). r43 makes them pay up front, pay back within 2 rounds for the
seat built around them, ask for fewer tags, and stop costing antimatter or burying better buildings. It also gives the
Capital engine a job, adds card throughput, cuts the tier-2 keep to 2 insight (1 upgraded), and deals 2 tier-2 cards in
the opening (Matt's amendment 4572). The card list with each card's reason and value check is in
[cards.md](cards.md). The committed-seat payback of every engine building, r42 and r43, is in [payback.md](payback.md).
The scripts are in this folder: `apply_r43.py` writes the content from r42's, `apply_powers.py` the two powers,
`notes_r43.py` the pricing notes, `card_list.py` the card list, and `probe_read.py` and `read.py` the reads.

## What changed

- **Engines pay up front (change 2).** Every engine building gives a one-time gain when built. A credit engine draws 3
  cards (4 on Foundation Model Campus and Vertical Monopoly). A credit gain on a credit cost would be a circular payment (guideline 5), and cards
  are the throughput change 8 asks for. A Power engine gains 2 energy, and a Security engine removes 2 troops. A
  threshold card arrives with its first tokens (Telemetry Archive, Retained Earnings, Open Assembly, Intrusion Lab,
  Conglomerate HQ), so its own play fills the first threshold.
- **Payback within 2 rounds (change 3).** `payback.py` assumes 1 card of the tag a round, 3 tags of any kind, 1.5
  plays at 10+ credits and 0.5 teammate plays. Give, less the when-built gain and the card's own first fire, over a
  round's yield, is now 1.8 rounds or less on every engine building. At r42, 20 of 34 were above 2, Substation Hub at
  6.7. Power to the People is the exception: it is a liberate capstone, priced at its liberation.
- **`needs` 2-3 (change 4).** Engine `needs` are now 2-3, where they were 3-5. The per-tag income programs need 2,
  and a building's per-tag income pays 3 a tag (it was 2). Zero-Day Auction stays a chain top at Cyber 5 and is much
  bigger: 1 antimatter per Cyber tag, 10c.
- **Antimatter (change 5).** Orbital Mass Driver and Recursive Self-Improvement pay its 7 in credits instead, and Dyson
  Swarm Tranche pays 2 energy.
- **Built over (change 6).** Five engines lost `replaces`: Foundation Model Campus, Orbital Mass Driver, Capital
  Projects Office, Hyperscale Botnet and Positron Storage Ring. Their owner now picks what to cover, or uses a free
  slot, so a tier-2 engine never has to bury a better building of its tag. Hyperscale Botnet is no longer a downgrade:
  3 insight a round, Cyber 2, draws, 14c. The four capstones that keep `replaces` (Orbital Embassy, Agency Directorate,
  Power to the People, Popular Uprising) still go over any building whose top card carries their tag.
- **Payroll Automation and the Capital engine (change 7).** Payroll Automation is now a feeder: add 4 Capital to one
  of your cards, and raise your credit income by 2. The copy op stays in the engine with no card. Retained Earnings
  adds 2 Capital a play and arrives with 2. Vendor Financing adds 5 Capital, Capital Injection 8, and Liquidity
  Event cashes Capital at 5 credits.
- **Credits pile up (change 8).** Cards cost more: tier-1 median 10 (r42: 9) and tier-2 median 16 (r42: 14), and
  six cards cost 5 or less, down from 17. Ten cheap cards got bigger effects at higher prices. Bridge Loan and
  Surplus Auction (0 credits, both made credits) were cut. Two new Action buildings turn spare resources into cards:
  Patent Library (3 credits for 2 cards) and Parts Exchange (2 chips for 2 cards). Two new powers do the same: the
  Builder's Procure (2 chips for 2 cards) and the Protector's Debrief (sacrifice a Diplomacy card for 2 cards). The
  powers are used 2.6-3.1 and 2.0-3.0 times a game on the dev seeds. The deck stays at 154 cards.
- **Keeps (change 1)** `keep_tier2` 2, `keep_tier2_upgraded` 1.
- **The opening (amendment 4572).** The opening deals 8 tier-1 and 2 tier-2 cards. A tier-2 card is kept for 1
  insight from round 1's 5, and takes none of the 4 free keeps. Value check: a tier-2 keep costs 2 insight (5 credits)
  in a draft from round 2, so the opening keep saves 2.5 credits a card. Keeping both still leaves 3 insight for round
  1's plays. With r42's opening restored by overlay, the new code plays every dev game the same (the code is neutral).
  On the dev seeds the opening moves the plain strategist 66.3 → 66.7 and the probe 38.5 → 42.0. No new agent code
  beyond the keep count: an opening tier-2 keep does not advance the free-keep count in `plan_keeps` or `keepable`.
- **The card fit.** Consulate Tower pays 9 credits each, not 12: its gain prints twice, and a two-digit number took
  its top box to 6.9 pt. Seven top boxes sit under 7.5 pt, as at r42: Agency Directorate, Retained Earnings, Intrusion
  Lab, Open Assembly, Popular Uprising, Conglomerate HQ and Joint Task Force. r43 left their top lines no longer than
  they were.

insight: a credit price for the second card a round, or credit-paid Actions. A flat sink stays the last resort.

## Speed

Bigger hands cost the agents. `per_decision.py` ran 16 games from seed 1000 on one thread, r42 → r43 (the frozen
build), on other games, beside a test run, so read the ratios. The strategist's options a decision rose 1.59×,
its decisions 1.23× and its ms a decision **1.80×** (2.21× a game). Greedy's options rose 1.44× and its ms a decision
1.19×. `speed.py check` read engine 2.68× and planner 2.27× a game, with NO VERDICT across the revision. The cost is the
card throughput (engines that draw) and the 2 opening tier-2 cards in hand. Before the draw step, r43 read 1.31× a
decision (8 games), so the draw step alone costs about a third more. The baseline is `--update`d for r43, so the next
check has a same-games reference. If the agents' cost matters more than the probe, the draw half is the lever to undo:
the fires half plus base r43 read −25 on the dev seeds.

## Play counts

The flagged cards and the new ones, r42 → r43, on the read's seeds: [play-counts.md](play-counts.md). Every
antimatter-freed card and every built-over card is now kept and played. Orbital Mass Driver goes from 0 to 0.23 a
game, Dyson Swarm Tranche from 0 to 0.12, Recursive Self-Improvement from 0.01 to 0.29, and Hyperscale Botnet from 0 to
0.12. The Capital engine plays too: Retained Earnings 0.07 → 0.35, Payroll Automation 0.02 → 0.10, Vendor Financing
0.01 → 0.15. Patent Library is played 0.27 a game and Parts Exchange 0.24. Two cards stay strong but rarely played:
Dispatch Control Room (0.08; offered 0.13 a game) and Liquidity Event (0.01).
