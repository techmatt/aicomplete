# 082 (r34, agents only): the value table and strategy agents

What the agents became and how they read. Rules and content stay at r34. Pairs: bp Builder + Protector, br Builder +
Researcher, pr Protector + Researcher; 2 players, d2, 48 seeds a cell unless noted. The read is one frozen build on
seeds 30700 (`experiments/screen-082*.json`), the judge confirmed on fresh seeds 30800 (`screen-082-fresh.json`).
Tuning ran on 31000 (`screen-082-tune*.json`), except the first two probes (`screen-082-probe*.json`, 24 seeds on
30700-30723, half the read's set: the draft's play-cost charge was picked there). Generated by `out/` scripts over each
run's output (not committed); the use tables by `python scripts/cards/use_read.py DIR`.

Agents: `engine` is greedy without strategy on the value table (the new-player tier); `strategist` is `engine:strategy=1`
(greedy with strategy, the judge candidate); the sweep before 082 is `sweep:plan_table=0,w.table=0,w.packet_keep=1` (the
screens' judge through 080: `sweep` follows the engine's defaults, so plain `sweep` is now on the table too); the engine
before 082 is `engine:plan_table=0,w.table=0,w.packet_keep=1`.

Against 078's (sweep, r33: nine cards over 10%, Orbital Observatory Array 44% first; never kept Foundation Model Campus,
Manifesto Leak, Summit Caucus) and 080's (no list; Popular Uprising kept in 62% and never played by the first r34 planner):

tokens gained 15-18 against 4-5). The cards left on the list, by cause:

- **Agent rules still missing.** Positron Storage Ring and Exabyte Archive (built over a building of their tag; the keep
  plan counts what they add over the one they cover, `plan_cover`, and they stay on the list): the heuristic values their
  income (energy, antimatter, Datasets) at its own weights, under the table's, so playing one over a standing building reads
  as a loss; a player would build them. Data Licensing Deal and Liquidity Event (cash a pile on one card): kept for a pile
  that never grew to the payout.
- **Game findings, a new player would skip them too.** Interceptor Battery: its Action (2 energy for a troop) nets −0.4 at
  the table's rates; Sample Repository (needs 3 Science for 1 cube a round) and Quantum Decryptor (an energy to play): small
  returns behind a gate.
- **Dear powers at 0, as at r33 (078).** Shelter (2 Security for one round's shelter of one country) and Rush Build (2
  Industry for a building's credits): seats leave about 10 credits unspent a round (078) and a shelter only pays in a round a
  strike lands, so burning two standing cards for either loses; a new player would skip both. Particle Run (2 Power for an
  antimatter): the Researcher rarely holds two Power cards; the rule "trade them when a card in hand needs the antimatter"
  (`antimatter_rule=1`, a probe) never fired in 288 games (31000). Game findings: the dear powers' price and their tag sit
  off the boards' engines. The progress term (`w.power_progress`) moved neither use nor wins (+2.8 ±3.3 off, 31000).
- **Grant** 1.7-2.1 a game (080: 1.6-1.9); **every liberate card is played**: Root Access 0.31-0.35 a game against 0.09-0.14
  before 082, Peacekeeping Mission 0.44-0.51 against 0.37-0.38, The Nuclear Option down to 0.02-0.04 (its 2 Discord Sway
  count −8 at the table) against 0.07-0.13.

## Where the planner with strategies first plays differently from the sweep

`planner:strategy=1` against `sweep` (both on the table), the same seeds: the first differing action is in round 1 in
every game, at the opening keep in 37 / 39 / 43 of 48 (bp / br / pr), else a round-1 power, prompt or pass; the sweep with
strategies (`sweep:strategy=1`) first differs at the same places. The strategies change what a seat keeps from its opening
10, and that is the whole first divergence; the four deals only matter after.
