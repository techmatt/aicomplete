# 098: rules r38, troop Sway off and dark aligned cities

Rules revision 38 (bumped from the constant, 37 to 38; the prompt also said 38). Both rules are candidates
(Matt's rulings, 2026-11-03).

## What changed

- **Removing an adversary troop no longer sways Unity** (`alignment.troop` 0, from 1). No card was given a printed
  Sway for it: none of the twelve troop-removal cards is about building Unity. Each is a Security card whose point is
  stopping a troop's harm (strikes, war, lost nodes), which is the double count Matt named. The Unity Bonus passive
  (`unity_bonus`, extra Sway a troop removal) still adds its amount; no card prints it today.
- **Dark aligned cities.** An adversary ambassador on an aligned city stays, and while it stands there the city
  gives its owner no energy (`board.city_dark_aligned` counts the city-rounds; `board.city_dark` stays neutral
  only). At r37 it diverted one round's energy and went. `board.ambassador_discord` (0) now sways per unit each
  round such a city is dark.
- **Agents:** `w.city_dark_aligned` 6 for each aligned city an adversary ambassador darkens, beside r37's
  `city_dark` 4 for a neutral one: the owner's free energy (about 4.5 credits) is lost for sure, where a neutral
  sale is near break-even.
- **Values** (`configs/agents/values.json`): a troop removed 11 (from 15: 1 Sway at `rates.sway` 4 less),
  7 in a country (from 10), a liberation's troop 3 (from 4).

## Calibration, reasoned before the r38 read

From the r37 binary on these seeds: an ambassador diverted an aligned city's energy 4.2-4.8 times a game, against
7.7-11.6 energy received from aligned cities. At r38 each first arrival stays, so the expectation was several dark
rounds per arrival, like neutral cities (5.8 a game at r37): about 6-9 dark aligned city-rounds, aligned energy down
by about a third, and more corruptions, as a standing ambassador is a step toward `corrupt_at`.

Measured: **about 3 dark aligned city-rounds a game** (2.8-3.4), under the estimate, and **aligned energy does not
fall** (d2 8.2 → 8.8, d0 10.8 → 11.7). The estimate missed that r37's 4.7 diversions were repeated arrivals on the
same cities, each costing a round; at r38 the later ones stack on the standing ambassador instead, and agents clear
their own cities (`city_dark_aligned` off: 4.2 dark rounds, aligned energy 7.2). The cost lands on the board
instead: **corruptions +1 a game** (d2 2.6 → 3.6, d0 1.9 → 2.6) and **nodes lost +2** (d2 8.1 → 10.7, d0 5.8 →
7.6). Nodes placed don't fall (d2 17.3 → 17.7, d0 18.3 → 19.2); dark neutral rounds and energy bought hold.
