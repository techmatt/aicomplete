## What changed

- **Slots by board:** Builder 5, Protector 4, Researcher 3 (r35 5 / 3 / 2). `ai.slots` and
  `ai.start_credits` became board-ordered lists (one value, or one per board in `content/ais.json`;
  `ai.rs` refuses other lengths). At r35's values the move replays 090's pair seeds game for game
  (`bench/same_results.py`: only the config hash and the spec's overlay text differ), c3 and c4
  included.
- **The premise:** the seat-ordered lists never gave a board the wrong slots or credits. The baseline
  seats B, P, R in board order; every pair spec from 080 to 092 restated all three lists in matching
  order (br `40,40 / 5,2`, pr `46,40 / 3,2`); c4's seat 3 is the Builder with 5. No script or e2e
  config sets `ai.boards` without them.
- **Popular Uprising:** the 2-Unity-tip gate is gone; the one-shot stays (liberate once, then discard
  the stack). The `tips` field stays in the schema, unused by any card.
- **Energy (amendment #3956):** city energy 5 credits (from 2); energy income valued at horizon ×
  4.5 × 0.8 (`rates.energy_income_share`, value.rs `income_rate`: income, income per tag and
  production); the tier-1 energy-income cards up 5 each; the two gifts become boosted conversions.
