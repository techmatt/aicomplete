# 127: no limits, fix the engines (r50 → r51)

Matt's ruling (2026-11-05): no resource has a holding limit and no global cap limits production; a runaway pile means a
runaway engine, fixed at card level. Revision 50 to 51, bumped from the constant (the prompt also said r51).
Tables: [fresh.md](fresh.md) (the final read), [dev.md](dev.md) (r50, r51 with no limits, r51 final on the dev seeds),
[disproof.md](disproof.md) (engines switched off), [levers.md](levers.md) (the card pass by lever). Overlay:
[overlay.txt](overlay.txt). Scripts: `read127.py` (wins, peaks, flags, credits, energy, plays, what fed each flagged
pile), `seat_read.py` (one seat-game), `engine_cards.py`, `production_read.py` (what a seat's credit production was
made of), `gen_disproof.py`, `gen_screen.py` (specs).

## What changed

- **The limits are gone.** That covers r49's 150 / 10 / 10 / 10 holding limits (`Cx::add_resource`), a threat
  stake coming back, every offer gated on room (a gain with no room, the ambassador project, selling a chip or a card,
  Withdraw, buying a city's energy) and the +60 cap on card credit production (`economy::raise_production`). Their
  counters (`economy.hold_lost*`, `production_lost.credits`, `board.completion_lost.energy`) went too.
- **The agents' limit rules are gone:** 124's `w.upkeep_loss`, `plan_limits`, `Ctx::room` / `with_room`, `Worth`'s
  gain and production room terms. The op values are pre-124's again. The strategist's cost a decision is 0.69× r50's
  (`per_decision.py`, 6 games), because the limit terms read the board's production at every evaluation.
- **The card pass** (16 cards, one overlay), priced below.
