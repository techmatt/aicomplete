# 106: the card overhaul (r42)

Engine overhaul part 2: the player cards as Terraforming Mars-style engines on 105's wording, erring toward strong
engines that look broken when played well (Matt, 2026-11-04). Every card, price and number is a candidate.

- **The card list:** [cards.md](cards.md): every new (28), changed (96) and cut (33) card with one line of reasoning,
  its value check at `configs/agents/values.json` and its loop cap. 30 cards are as r41 printed them. The per-tag
  designers' working papers, with their flags, are [proposals/](proposals/); integration edits are
  [merge-notes.json](merge-notes.json) (they override a proposal row in cards.md).
- **Play counts:** [play-counts.md](play-counts.md) (offered / kept / played per game, every new and changed card).
- **How:** one designer per tag on [brief.md](brief.md), one adversarial reviewer per two tags fixing in place, then
  integration here.

## The deck

154 cards (from 159): 109 tier 1, 45 tier 2; programs 53%, buildings 31%, events 16% (r41: 42 / 42 / 17; target 60 /
25 / 15). Median printed cost 10 (r41: 7): tier 1 median 9, tier 2 median 14; Gigafactory Charter is "40 credits,
+12 credit income". Each tag is primary on 25-26 cards.

- **Income moved to programs.** Every flat-income building became a program ("raise your credit income by N"):
  Robotic Foundry, Arcology Foundry, Fusion Startup Stake, Dyson Swarm Tranche, Hardened Bunker Complex, Sample
  Repository and kin. Baseload Contract is +2 energy income (20 credits), with Pumped Hydro Storage, Homeland Security
  Act and Multiyear Defense Budget as its kin.
- **Per-tag income, one shape per tag:** six programs, "raise your credit income by 1 per X tag on your cards", needs 3
  of X, 10 credits (Research Royalties, Economies of Scale, Ransomware Syndicate, Capacity Market, Peace Dividend,
  Defense Appropriations); building versions pay 2 a tag (Foundation Model Campus, Vertical Monopoly).
- **Triggers, one engine per tag in each tier.** Tier 1, "when you play an X card": Citation Engine and Telemetry
  Archive (Science), Retained Earnings (Industry), Exploit Broker (Cyber), Dispatch Control Room (Power), Open Assembly
  (Diplomacy), Agency Field Station (Security). Tier 2, every tag on the played card (`play_tag`, no tag): Interdisciplinary
  Institute, Conglomerate Headquarters, Command-and-Control Grid, Smart Grid Backbone, Orbital Embassy, Agency
  Directorate.
- **The other agreed ideas:** token buildings fed per play (Telemetry Archive, Retained Earnings, Open Assembly,
  Conglomerate Headquarters, Agency Directorate); discounts on X cards (Darknet Exchange 5 credits off Cyber, Grad
  Student Pool 4 off Science, Hyperscale Botnet 1 insight off every card); rebates (Lights-Out Assembly at 10+, Capital
  Projects Office); copy (Payroll Automation); pay for fighting (Security Contractor HQ troops, Security Ops Floor and
  Intrusion Lab compute, Honeypot Relay and Reconciliation Office ambassadors); teammate triggers (Consulate Tower,
  Substation Hub, Joint Task Force); antimatter by threshold (Intrusion Lab, Positron Storage Ring) and per tag (Zero-Day
  Auction); reuse (Simulation Cluster, Quick Reaction Force).
- **Popular Uprising** no longer leaves play: it liberates every third round at most (once-a-round Action, 6
  Followers a liberation, cities in reach). The deliberate "broken when played well" capstone.

## Decisions at integration

- **Three op extensions** (Matt's default, ask 1, no answer: 105-wording gaps): `production_per_tag` (a program's
  per-tag income raise, counted once), `income_per_tag` `amount` (2 per tag), `play_tag` with no tag (every tag; an
  empty-ring mark, `#any`, added to the viewer's icons and the Legend). Each with its face text, value arm, values.json
  row (`production_per_tag`, `rates.tags_per_card` 1.45), ledger mirror and an engine test.
- **105 face defects fixed:** "a Industry card" → "an Industry card"; "when you remove compute from an adversary
  project" → "when you remove adversary compute" (compute only lives on projects; it didn't fit 7.5 pt).
- **Restored:** Synthetic Data Forge (Telemetry Archive was left the only Dataset holder, so Dataset Scraper and Data
  Licensing Deal were dead without it) and Botnet Takedown (the cuts took botnet removers from 4 to 2: Singularity
  Exploit, Root Access; that is the adversary's counterplay, not an engine).
- **Consular Pouch needs 2 Diplomacy:** a program whose only effect is a per-tag gain is refused with none of its tag
  already in play (`effects/feasible.rs`), though it would count itself; the `needs` makes that visible on the card.
- **Varied:** three cards printed "+8 credit income": University Spinout stays; Cryptojacking Campaign pays 2 energy for
  +9; Multiyear Defense Budget is +6 credits and +1 energy for 30.
- **Card fit:** Singularity Exploit back to r41's effects (4 credits); Peacekeeper Rotation removes 2 troops only;
  Agency Field Station pays directly (remove 1 troop a Security card); Agency Field Station and Open Assembly print "an X
  card" not "per X tag" (no card carries a tag twice). **Recorded exceptions** (top box under 7.5 pt): Agency Directorate
  5.4 (a per-tag counter with a country removal: two clauses no house phrase shortens), Retained Earnings, Intrusion Lab,
  Open Assembly, Popular Uprising 6.3, Conglomerate Headquarters, Joint Task Force 6.9.
- **Board incomes unchanged.** A +3 printed-credit raise on each board (28 / 24 / 21) read +11.1 ±4.6 for the
  strategist on the read's seeds (r42 with the raise against r42 without), but credits already pile up at the end
  (below), the cards alone more than undo 105's drop, and Matt plans to lower the incomes later.

## Play counts and the strong cards the agents leave (for 107)

29 of the 124 new or changed cards are played under 0.05 a game by the strategist. Most are engines behind a `needs`
of 3-5 of one tag or built over their tag: Citation Engine (needs 3 Science), Foundation Model Campus (over Science,
needs 4), Recursive Self-Improvement, Security Contractor HQ, Orbital Mass Driver and Hyperscale Botnet (over their tag;
Hyperscale is kept 0.15-0.31 a game and never played), Dyson Swarm Tranche, Continental Supergrid, Tidal Barrage, Smart
Grid Backbone, Dispatch Control Room, Conglomerate Headquarters, Retained Earnings, Power to the People, Positron
Storage Ring. The agents keep a tag mix and don't build tag depth toward a requirement, so a chain's top is dead in
their hands. **Payroll Automation** is kept 0.33-0.42 and played 0.02: with income moved to programs, few buildings pay
an income to copy (the over-X tier-2 ones and the 2-a-tag pair), so it waits on a combo the agents never assemble. The
cards that carry the rise are the big flat-income programs and the fight cards: Multiyear Defense Budget,
Counterinsurgency Campaign, Gigafactory Charter, Robotic Foundry, Honeypot Relay, Agency Field Station, Diplomatic
Expulsion, Martial Cordon (0.35-0.5 a game).

The tier-2 every-tag triggers read negative for the strategist: it keeps them (0.2-0.4 a game, 6.5 insight each)
and plays few (Conglomerate Headquarters and Smart Grid Backbone 0.01), so they cost keeps. A finding for 107 (the
keep plan prices a trigger at the table's generic rate), not a reason to weaken them. `play_read.py`: the strategist
keeps Hyperscale Botnet (26% of games), Capacitor Discharge (10%) and Plant Overdrive (2%) and never plays them, and
never keeps the Capital cards (Retained Earnings, Capital Injection, Collateralized Grid, Liquidity Event, Vendor
Financing): the Capital engine is dead to the agents.

## Loops

Every loop a card adds is traced in its row of cards.md. The ones worth naming: credits → cards → per-tag income is
capped by the hand, insight and keeps (a program's per-tag raise counts once); troop removal → Security Contractor HQ
credits → more removal is capped by the troops on the board and once-a-round Actions (Quick Reaction Force reuses one);
Datasets → Data Licensing Deal credits places no tokens (`Content::check` refuses a place-token trigger that places
tokens); a reuse never reuses a reuse; Popular Uprising's repeat liberation is capped by its Action and 6 Followers.
