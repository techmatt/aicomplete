# 106 Diplomacy section (proposal)

There are 26 cards, down from 28. By tier: 18 tier 1 and 8 tier 2. By kind: 15 programs, 7 buildings and 4 events (review: Liaison Corps went from building to program; 7 buildings, one over the target, because the discount, the fight trigger, the token engine, the teammate trigger, the typed Action counter and the two over-X capstones all need a standing piece). Median printed cost is 10 (tier 1 median 9; tier 2 costs 13-42). Five cards are cut and three are new.

**How the rates are applied.** Credit 1, insight 2.5, ambassador 4, Sway 4, Follower fed 2.5, energy 4.5, antimatter 7. The keep is 2.5 at tier 1 and 6.5 at tier 2. "Pay" means credits + 2.5 × insight + keep (+ other costs). Income is counted over 4 rounds at tier 1 and 2.5 at tier 2. A left tag counts 1, plus 0.5 for each further tag.

**Seat fire rates.** Engines are priced at the rate a seat built on Diplomacy would see:
- About 1.2 Diplomacy cards a round, about 1.3 Diplomacy tags a round.
- About 1.2 ambassador removals a round. The table assumes 0.8.
- Three seats, so 2 teammates × 1.5 plays = 3 teammate plays a round. Of those, about 0.7 are Diplomacy plays.

"Ledger" below is `effect_ledger.py`'s generic `reasoned` value at the table's own fire rates. The ledger can't read `peace_dividend` (production_per_tag) or `orbital_embassy` (untagged play_tag), so those two are priced by hand only.

**Where the assigned ideas sit:**
- **Teammate triggers:** Consulate Tower ("you both gain"). (Liaison Corps' untagged teammate trigger was cut in review: it fired on about 3 plays a round whatever you did, so it was flat income on a building, which Matt's direction moves to programs.)
- **Paid for fighting:** Reconciliation Office.
- **Followers per Diplomacy tag, with a payoff:** Open Assembly. Its trigger is also the tier-1 Diplomacy trigger engine.
- **Per-tag income program:** Peace Dividend.
- **Tier-2 trigger engine:** Orbital Embassy, which counts every tag on a played card.
- **Discount:** Protocol Office.

**Notes for the integrator:**
- **Stale doc lines.** `docs/rules/card-grammar.md` names Embassy Compound (move_one, range 2), Liberation Accords (clear 6) and Back-Channel Talks (`only` factories; still true).
- **Popular Uprising.** It loses `once`, so the liberate-capstones line in player-cards.md may need its wording checked.
- **Value table rows.** The `when` row in values.json needs the untagged play_tag. The `production` row needs production_per_tag.
- **Agents.** Consulate Tower's `team_play` fire on another seat's plays is priced by the eval at `fires_play`; check it at 3 seats.
- **Text fit (review).** Open Assembly's Effect reads about 100 characters ("when you play a card, add 1 Follower here per Diplomacy tag on it; at 3, sway 3 toward Unity"), over the ~80 building box. It needs `npm run cards:fit`; if it fails, the fix is a shorter `play_tag` phrase in `face.rs` (it hits Agency Field Station and Telemetry Archive the same way).
