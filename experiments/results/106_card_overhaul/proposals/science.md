# 106 Science section (proposal)

There are 25 cards. Tier 1 has 18 (11 programs, 4 buildings, 3 events). Tier 2 has 7 (2 programs, 4 buildings, 1 event). Totals: 13 programs, 8 buildings, 4 events. The median printed cost is 9. The big cards are University Spinout (24, tier 1), Foundation Model Campus (20, tier 2) and Forensic Audit (27, the capstone). From the current 30 cards, 8 are cut and 3 are new.

Prices use `configs/agents/values.json`. Pay = credits + insight × 2.5 + keep (2.5 for tier 1, 6.5 for tier 2) + other costs. Income counts for 4 rounds on tier 1 and 2.5 on tier 2. Engines are priced at the Researcher's rate: about 1.2 Science cards a round (no card carries a tag twice, so that is also 1.2 Science tags a round), about 3 tags of any kind a round, and 6 Science tags in play by mid-game. Where the table's generic rate gives a different answer, that is noted. The effect ledger was run on the 22 cards it can price. Its generic `fair_credits` reads Citation Engine at 6 and Data Licensing Deal at 7, because it fires them at the table's generic rate. Both are seat engines and are priced here at the seat rate on purpose.

## Flags for the merge

1. **Grad Student Pool is a credit discount, not an insight one (review fix).** In `price.rs`, `floored` applies `cards.discount_floor` (1) to insight, and the draft already takes 1 insight off per `science_every` (2) Science tags, so a seat with 2 or more Science tags plays every Science card printed at insight 2 or less for 1 insight: an insight discount on Science cards was worth about 0 to the Researcher (below 0 net, a bug). The reviewer took option (b): "Your Science cards cost 4 credits less to play", at 8 credits. If the Science section must carry an insight-discount idea, it needs a rules change outside these files (a floor of 0 for `card_discount` insight); Cyber's Hyperscale Botnet carries the every-card insight discount.
2. **There are 8 buildings against the target of about 6.** Seven of the agreed ideas need standing pieces: a trigger, a threshold, a discount, a reuse, a recounted per-tag income, a tag-counting trigger and a token payoff. Orbital Observatory Array stays as the Action that Simulation Cluster reuses within Science. Programs come to 13 against the target of 15.
3. **Text fit (review fix).** Telemetry Archive's per-tag box ("when you play a card, add 1 Dataset here per Science tag on it; at 3, gain 5 insight", about 93 characters) ran past 80. No card carries a tag twice, so `play_tag` science fires exactly as `play` science does; it now prints the `play` form, which today's face fits at 80 characters ("when you play a Science card, add 2 Datasets here; at 4, gain 5 insight"). `npm run cards:fit` still has to confirm the set.
4. **Threshold timing.** In `triggers.rs` the threshold is checked only after the card's own trigger fires, and it uses a `while` loop. Datasets that Dataset Scraper puts on Telemetry Archive wait for the next Science card, then pay out every full 4 at once.
5. **Three cards use the item's new extension ops and are priced by hand:** Research Royalties (`production_per_tag`), Foundation Model Campus (`income_per_tag` amount 2) and Interdisciplinary Institute (`play_tag` with no tag).
6. **Datasets have one holder.** With Synthetic Data Forge and Exabyte Archive cut and Grad Student Pool no longer a token card, Telemetry Archive is the only card in the deck holding Datasets. Dataset Scraper and Data Licensing Deal are dead without it in play. The merge should check play counts for the two (the agents' keep plan must see the dependency).
7. **Near-duplicate across sections:** University Spinout (24, raise credit income by 8) prints the same effect as Cyber's Cryptojacking Campaign (22 + 2 insight) and Security's Multiyear Defense Budget (24). Three flat "+8 credit income" programs: the merge should keep one or vary them.

## The engines when played well

- **Credits from tags:** Research Royalties at 6 tags is +6 a round, permanently. Foundation Model Campus then pays 2 a round for every Science tag. Citation Engine pays 6 for every Science card, and Grad Student Pool takes 4 off it. Interdisciplinary Institute pays 1 insight for every tag. By round 6, a Science/Cyber card played on this board pays 6 credits and 2 insight at once and raises the Campus by 2 a round.
- **Datasets:** each Science card adds 2 Datasets to Telemetry Archive, each paying 5 credits through Data Licensing Deal. Every fourth Dataset gives 5 insight. A Dataset Scraper adds 20 credits at once and fills the next payout.
- **Draw:** Orbital Observatory Array draws 3 for 2 credits, and Simulation Cluster reuses it for 6 cards a round with no keep paid. Those draws feed the play triggers above.
