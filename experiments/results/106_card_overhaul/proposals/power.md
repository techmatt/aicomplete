# 106 Power section (proposal)

25 cards (from 24): 17 tier 1 / 8 tier 2; 15 programs, 6 buildings, 4 events. Four new, three cut. Printed credit costs: median 16, from 0 to 30.

Rates are from `values.json`: energy 4.5, energy income x 0.8 (T1 +1 = 14.4, T2 +1 = 9), credit income 4 (T1) / 2.5 (T2), antimatter 7, node 4 (free 6), insight 2.5, keep 2.5 / 6.5, tags 1 + 0.5. "Pay" counts the credits, the insight x 2.5, the keep and any other cost.

**Seat rates for engines.** A Power seat plays about 1 Power card a round, counts 4-6 Power tags by mid-game, and plays about 1.5 cards a round with about 1.6 tags each (2.4 tags a round of any kind). Its teammates play about 0.5 Power cards a round between them. The table's generic rate is 0.45 tagged plays a round.

**Why the median is above 10.** Energy income is the section's job, and +1 is worth 14.4 on a tier-1 card, so moving every flat-income card to a program puts most of the section at 12-24. The deck median comes down through the other sections. The ledger (`effect_ledger.py`, run on the 21 cards that use no new op) gives fair prices within about 3 of the printed ones at the table's generic rates. The exceptions are the engines, which the ledger prices at the generic fire rate: Dispatch Control Room, which it puts at 6.75 against the printed 12, and Positron Storage Ring, at 14.9 against 16.

**New ops used** (priced by hand). `production_per_tag` is on Tidal Barrage, Capacity Market and Continental Supergrid. `play_tag` with no tag is on Smart Grid Backbone.

## The agreed ideas, by card

- **Flat energy income at a real rate:** Baseload Contract (+2), and its kin Fusion Startup Stake, Peaker Turbines, Pumped Hydro Storage, Faraday Yard, Dyson Swarm Tranche and Continental Supergrid.
- **Per-tag energy as a program:** Tidal Barrage. Capacity Market and Continental Supergrid are its credit twins.
- **Teammate trigger, Power-first:** Substation Hub.
- **Tier-1 Power trigger engine:** Dispatch Control Room. **Tier-2:** Smart Grid Backbone, with no tag, so it counts every tag.
- **Toward antimatter:** Positron Storage Ring, over-X on Dispatch, which feeds Dyson and The Nuclear Option. A threshold isn't printable on Power because it has no tokens.
- **Flat-income buildings moved to programs:** Faraday Yard, Tidal Barrage, Continental Supergrid and Dyson Swarm Tranche. Substation Hub instead becomes the teammate trigger, and its income goes.

## For whoever merges

- Grid-Tied Smelter is cut in Industry's proposal, so Substation Hub is the deck's only Power teammate trigger.
- Industry's Lights-Out Assembly (rebate at 10 or more, 4 credits) and Capital Projects Office (at 10 or more, 8) fire on most Power cards: the section's median is 16.
- **Median 16 against the target near 10:** not fixed in review. Energy income priced at 14.4 a step puts the flat-income programs at 18-24; lowering them would push them well past "err strong". The deck median has to come down in the other sections.
- The energy flood comes from Smart Grid Backbone, Tidal Barrage and Beamed Power Constellation. Read held energy at the end and Demand Response's uses per game in the first smoke.
