## The rule (apply_prices.py)

The anchor ratio is get ÷ pay, get taken from 111's anchor check (its cards.md, at the r44 effect; a range's
midpoint), pay = credits + 4 × (keep + play insight) + other costs at the anchors.

- **A card 111 lowered** (credits or play insight; 76 cards) goes back to its r43 price: 59 cards.
- **Under 1.0 at r43's price** with its r44 effect (17 cards): 111's cut was partly fair (often its effect had been cut
  too, as Breach and Clear's), so it takes the highest price between r44 and r43 that reads at least 1.0, r43's play
  insight first. The typed-jam buildings (Civic Watch, Garrison Command, Market Watchdog, Sentinel Mesh) go back to 7
  credits but keep 0 insight; Breach and Clear 6, Force Protection Order 6, Peacekeeping Mission 41.
- **Over the maximum** (tier 1 over 1.5, tier 2 over 2.5), credits rise to read the maximum: Lights-Out Assembly 11 → 14
  (1.71), Reconciliation Office 14 → 17 (1.81), Security Ops Floor 12 → 13 (1.55), Moonshot Grant 22 → 23 (1.53). No
  tier-2 card is over 2.5.
- **Not raised above r43:** the prompt allows "higher where the anchors show it fair at a higher price". Every tier-1
  card restored reads 1.0-1.45 at r43's price, so none goes higher. Eight tier-2 cards read 1.55-1.96 at r43's price
  (Deep State Ledger, Dyson Swarm Tranche, Recursive Self-Improvement, Kinetic Interdiction, Killswitch Directive,
  Zero-Day Auction, Smart Grid Backbone, Exodus Corridors); they stay at r43, since tier 2 is efficiency (guideline 23)
  and 2.5 is its maximum. Taking them to 1.5 would have put the tier-2 median past 20.
- Cards with no anchor line in 111's table (kept unchanged by 111; 22) were not re-read.

Result: tier-1 median 10 (r44 9, r43 10), tier-2 median 18 (r44 14, r43 16; 111 raised and added some tier-2 cards),
5 cards at 5 credits or less (r44 20, r43 6). The liberate capstones are 18-42 credits and read 1.0-1.35, inside the
margin: no longer an exception.
