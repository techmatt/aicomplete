## What changed

- **D130, threats take their own site only at tier 2.** Agitators, Embargo and Insider Access create 1 agent (were
  3, 2, 2): with the uncountered anchor agent (`threat_agents` 1) that is 2, under every site's number at every level
  (city 5, 3 from d1; station and factory 3). Tier-2 Insurrection, Trade Blockade, Garrison State, Rootkit and
  Station Seizure still reach it: the capture is now their counted effect (`docs/harms.md`, *Threats that take their
  own site*, generated by `harms.rs` `captures`; `aic-sim docs --check` and `drafts/adversary/check.py` refuse a
  tier-1 one; the tier-2 drafts name the harm). 285's option (a) read "2 on a city": 2 + the anchor takes a city from
  level 1, so 1 (design question 1, defaulted).
- **D132, a located line acts only on its own kind of hex.** Offensive deals its tier-2 threat's first token onto a
  node hex; when that hex is not the kind its first location names, `place_first_at` marks it
  (`threats.loc.<id>.off`) and every line placing on it does nothing: Garrison State's agents, Iron Curtain's and
  Manhunt's troop, Total War's and Conscription's troops, Siege's destruction, Casus Belli's war. A line measured
  from it (Arsenal's factory "nearest here", Fallout next to it, a botnet's nearest other piece) still measures from
  it (the diff review's catch: Arsenal's factory went to the map's lowest-numbered zone).
- **D121, D128, refused powers.** A two-node Growth option needs a free hex for its second node once the first is
  placed (`board::second_node_site`); Patrol II, whose extra energy buys only its hardening, needs a facility to
  harden on or next to a target (a group sibling with the same effects less the hardening: Fortify II, which also
  removes a second troop, is unaffected).
- **D131.** The threats Sleeper Cells and War Economy are **Rootkit** and **Arsenal** (ids kept; the level names
  stay), checked against every card, threat, event, project and level name.
- **Not play:** D124, the seat strip's income adds production's factory chips and station cubes
  (`board::production_income`); D125, a node lost to agents logs once; D122, `burn_late` retired (no board power
  burns since r131); D126, card-pricing.md and player-cards.md state payback as `payback.py` measures it (15 of 38
  engine buildings within 2 rounds, 28 within 3, median 2.3; eight at 5 or more).

## For new players (r137)

- A tier-1 threat left unpaid never takes its own site by itself: its new agent and the one every unpaid threat
  lands make 2, under any site's number. A tier-2 threat that creates agents can take its city or station.
- A threat dealt onto your node hex by a Discord card's Offensive does nothing *on* that hex unless it is the kind
  of place the threat names; it still counts as near you.
- Growth's two-node option and Patrol II are offered only when all they pay for can happen.
- Rootkit and Arsenal are threats; Sleeper Cells and War Economy are difficulty levels.
