# 130: late energy sinks (r52 → r53)

Matt's rulings (design doc *Economy*, revs 21-24): energy sinks are a secondary cost on power-hungry titles, uncommon,
with bigger late sinks on top ("an Action 'spend X energy: Y effect', or a tier-2 building with a very big energy cost
and a very strong effect"); no holding or production limits, and unspent energy converts to nothing; unplayed cards
get stronger, not cheaper, and the anchors set the price. Revision 52 to 53, bumped from the constant (the prompt also
said r53). Tables: [fresh.md](fresh.md) (the final read), [dev.md](dev.md), [deck_rates.md](deck_rates.md) (every
card's plays per card in hand, r52 and r53 dev). The card pass: [overlay.txt](overlay.txt). Scripts: `read130.py`,
`deck_rates.py`, `short_read.py`, `energy_sources.py`, `flag_sinks.py`, `peak_vs_held.py`. Screens: `experiments/screen-130-exascale.json`,
`-exascale2.json`, `-sinks.json`.

**Why the flags hold** (dev r53, strategist Builder): the flag reads the energy *peak*, which forms at production,
before the action phase spends it. A flagged Builder gains 19 energy a game from cities against 10 for the rest
(`energy_sources.py`), and a Builder that played a sink is flagged as often as one that played none (32 of 114 against
20 of 78; `flag_sinks.py`): its sinks take about 1 use a game. Holographic Hall stood 133 Builder rounds: used 39,
skipped 21 with 5.6 energy held (the heuristic's call), not offered 73 with 0.9 held (the energy already went to node
powers). More sinks would need the Builder to end its action phases with energy left, or the agents to spend at 5+.

**The same rule on the energy held after the action phase** (`peak_vs_held.py`: the most a seat held when an action
phase ended, rising over its start by more than 3 x its income a round and more than 10, the pile with nowhere to
go): Builder dev 14 → 8 of 192, fresh 9 → 3 of 96; a flagged Builder's peak 18.4 → 16.3 (dev) and 21.3 → 16.0
(fresh), the most it held after an action phase 10.5 → 8.6 and 14.4 → 7.7. Small counts, but this is where the sinks
show: most of a flagged peak is spent in its own round on node powers.

## New-player summary

Particle Accelerator's Action can now take its 3 research cubes onto your own board instead of a megaproject, so it
still works once the megaprojects are done. Four new cards spend spare energy: Directed-Energy Array (pay 2 energy:
remove 3 adversary ambassadors in reach), Holographic Assembly Hall (pay 2 energy: 2 Sway toward Unity and 2
ambassadors), Wafer Fab Line (pay 2 energy: draw 2 cards and 2 chips), each once a round, and the tier-2 Exascale
Training Cluster (8 energy to build: 6 research cubes, then +3 insight every round). Positron Storage Ring draws 2
cards when built. "An open megaproject" means one that isn't complete.
