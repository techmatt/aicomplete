## The premise moved: the pile is the last round's income

"Credits left at game end" (`margin.unspent`) is what a seat held when its last action phase ended, or now if the
game ended inside one. A Unity-deck win ends the game inside a round, before that round's upkeep, so a won game
ends holding the previous upkeep's income ([end_credits.py](end_credits.py), strategist, team credits a seat):

| build | won games (Unity deck) end on | held after every completed action phase (all games) | lost games end on |
|---|---|---|---|
| r45 | 62 (76 games) | 9.5 | 7-9 |
| income only | 60 (74) | 9.0 | |
| r46 | 62 (71) | 8.4 | 7 |

Seats already spend down to 8-11 credits every round; the 41-51 a seat is late income in won games. The mean
falls when late income or the win rate falls, and here most of the fall is fewer wins. Greedy reads the same way
(won games 27 → 19 a seat; held 5.1 → 4.2). The prompt's "late in the game nothing in hand is worth buying" is not
what the measure shows. docs/experiments.md now says so beside the measure. Asked Matt (design, default taken): keep
18 / 16 / 14 and report, rather than cut income further to move a metric that tracks wins.
