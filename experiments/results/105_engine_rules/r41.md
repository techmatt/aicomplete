# 105: the engine wording and the insight economy (r41)

The rules the new engine cards need (106 rewrites the cards), one demo card each, and the insight economy. Rules:
[card-grammar.md](../../../docs/rules/card-grammar.md) (*The engine wording*), [ai-powers.md](../../../docs/rules/ai-powers.md)
(*Insight conversions*), [economy.md](../../../docs/rules/economy.md), [threats.md](../../../docs/rules/threats.md). Play
counts: [play-counts.md](play-counts.md).

## What was built

- **One standing op for every trigger,** `when { on: <event>, then: [instant ops] }`, an Effect (`:`). Events: `play`
  (a tag or any), `play_tag` (per tag on the played card), `team_play` (another player; `both`), `play_cost` (a
  rebate on the printed cost), `remove_troop` (once a troop), `remove_compute` and `remove_ambassadors` (once an
  action), `place_tokens` (once a token; P1), `wall_stops` (Vigil). Plays fire after the card resolves, every seat's
  triggers in seat order; a building counts itself. Fight triggers diff the board's troops, ambassadors and project
  compute around the one action a seat takes (a card, an Action, a power, a converter, a standard action), so nested
  resolutions (Rush Build's building, a reused Action) don't fire twice.
- `threshold { at, then }`: whenever a trigger leaves `at` or more tokens here, `at` come off and `then` resolves.
  "At N" is a house term (the face says "at 3, gain 4 insight": "remove them" did not fit 7.5 pt).
- `card_discount { tag?, resource, amount }`: credits or insight off a card's play, every card kind, through
  `Prices` (so `play_cost` and `play_insight` read it), at `discount_floor`.
- `copy_income` (a program), `reuse` (an Action; never one whose Action is a reuse; only an Action used this round),
  `discard_hand` (Synthesis).
- New marks: `play` (a card you play), `≥N`. Legend rows in card-guidelines.md.
- The cube converter: standard action `insight`, 1 cube for `board.cube_insight` (1) insight, no limit. Converters
  carry `once`; only Onyx's is set.

## Loops

- **Onyx (kept once a round):** 1 cube → 2 chips → a 2-chip station → 1 cube a round once its country's card is
  complete: unlimited, each cube would be a station the next round, doubling the cubes, capped only by open hexes in
  reach. The cap is the once a round.
- **Horizon, Biomia, Cintamani (limit dropped):** none feeds back. Horizon's spends 2 energy a use (energy is the scarce
  resource); Biomia's turns 2 cubes into 1 Sway, a team clock, not a resource; Cintamani's makes insight, which buys
  cards. Cube cards (Sample Repository, Preprint Exchange) are capped by the cards.
- **Fieldwork + Synthesis:** energy → cards → insight, once a round each.
- **Triggers:** a trigger's gain never fires the trigger that paid it, but a place-token trigger whose ops place tokens
  would loop: `Content::check` refuses one. Two
  reuse buildings can't loop: a reuse never reuses a reuse.
- **Vigil:** capped by the troops the adversary marches next to the Protector's nodes, once a troop a march.

## The ballpark read

Final build, strategist, r41 against r40 on 33500-33547 (48 a cell, paired): bp 21 vs 21 (0.0 ±6.7), br 42 vs 56
(−14.6 ±8.9), pr 25 vs 50 (−25.0 ±8.2); pooled −13.2 ±4.6. Greedy 4 / 4 / 4 vs 2 / 2 / 4. Replayed on the commit build:
`same_results.py` same 288 games.

**Speed** (paired `per_decision.py`, ratios on a busy box): strategist 1.02× a decision (c2), 1.13× (c3, the
Researcher seated); planner 1.35× (c2). With 105's content reverted by overlay the new code costs the planner about
1.06× a decision, so the rest is the r41 rules (more insight, plays and conversions to search). Two hot-path cuts went
in (a program's price and the play triggers read no tracks or prices unless the seat holds a discount or a trigger).
The speed baseline was not moved.

### The first read

The first read (before the demo repricing, superseded) fell against r40: strategist bp 15 vs 21, br 33 vs 56, pr 33
vs 50. A diagnostic add-one-back factorial on the read seeds (each part of 105 added alone to the r40-like overlay set,
48 a cell, so about ±10 a cell) put most of it on the **insight counters** (bp -8, br -29, pr -27): the agents hold
insight at 0.5 a point (the Patience seat at 1.0) and rarely have 5 left, so those four threats execute more
(`threats.executed` 2.2 vs 1.4 a game in pr). Synthesis (br -10, pr -19) and the cube converters (pr -15) read negative
in the Researcher pairs, where the Researcher's banked insight doubled (`economy.insight_carried`). Vigil read +12 in bp,
the extra insight +10. Reported, not tuned (Matt, message 4397).
