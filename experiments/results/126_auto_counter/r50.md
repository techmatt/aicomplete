# 126: `auto_counter` priced from play (r50, agents only)

The value table's `auto_counter` row (`configs/agents/values.json`) assumed an auto-counter fires about 0.5 times a
round (121 raised it from 0.3 when r48 widened the reach to any country). Measured in play at r50 it fires 0.30 times a
round. The row goes 2.5 → 1.5 a point (0.3 fires × 5). No rules, card or price change; no `RULES_REVISION` bump.

## The measure

`threats.auto_countered` is one per-game total of points, with no seat, so it can't give fires per holder. The measure
build adds sim-only counters in `threats::try_complete` ([measures.patch](measures.patch), not committed):

- `m126.fires`: a countered threat with a standing auto-counter;
- `m126.fires.seat<s>`: a fire that seat's auto-counter took part in;
- `m126.cut.<resource>`: the cut by resource.

It is play-neutral: `same_results.py` on main's binary against the patched one: same, 864 games. Holder-rounds run
from the round the card was played to the game's last, both counted. A play's round is read from the action stream
([read126.py](read126.py)).

Dev seeds 35200-35343, the three pairs at d2, 144 a cell, main 9c37655 (r50, the row at 2.5):

| agent | board | holders | fires a holder-round ± SE | credits a fire |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| strategist | Builder | 29 | 0.378 ± 0.058 | 9.24 |
| strategist | Protector | 24 | 0.309 ± 0.060 | 9.48 |
| strategist | Researcher | 44 | 0.242 ± 0.038 | 7.73 |
| strategist | all | 97 | **0.299 ± 0.029** | 8.73 |
| greedy | all | 4 | 0.625 ± 0.179 | 11.10 |

- **Per card:** only Autonomous Defense Grid (cyber, 5) is in the baseline deck. Market Stabilization Board (economic,
  5) is in `cards.json` but not dealt, so it has no rate. It shares the row.
- **Per board:** the Builder's holders see more fires than the Researcher's (0.38 against 0.24, a gap of 0.14 ±
  0.07, about 2 SE). The on arm repeats the order (0.40 / 0.34 / 0.21). I kept one number. The gap is under 2× and
  only about 2 SE. A per-board form would need the seat's board in the value arm, and nothing else in the table
  prices by board. Greedy all but never builds the card (4 holders in 432 games).
- **The rate holds at the new price:** on the on arm the strategist's holders read 0.316 ± 0.042. So the rate isn't
  something the 2.5 price picked.

**The other factor (flag, not taken).** The row prices a fire at 5 credits a printed point (25 for ADG's 5). The cut
a fire takes off, priced at the table's resource rates, is 8.7 credits a fire, about 1.75 a printed point:

- 1.9 credits, 0.12 energy and 2.5 insight a fire;
- the threats cut are 7-credit, 5-insight and 1-2-energy cyber threats.

At that worth the row would be 0.52, not 1.5. The cut doesn't count a threat it turned from executed into countered
(harm avoided), so I left the ×5 alone. The prompt asks for the fire rate.

## Recipe

- Specs: `experiments/screen-126-dev.json` (both arms), `experiments/screen-126-r50.json` (fresh).
- The arms are two binaries on the same configs, since the table is compiled in (`include_str!`).
- The measure: `patch -p1 < experiments/results/126_auto_counter/measures.patch` on the build you're measuring.
- Read: `python experiments/results/126_auto_counter/read126.py OFF=off ON=on [--measure]`.
- Each run is about 90 s on 6 threads (864 games).
