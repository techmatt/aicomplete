# Rules history

One line per rules revision, newest first: the item, what changed, and its status.
[rules/](rules/README.md) is the current rules and marks each rule **locked** or a candidate;
a revision's reasoning and numbers are in its write-up under `experiments/results/` and in git.
Every rules change adds its line here in the same commit (CLAUDE.md).

**Older names:** rows through r34 say *presence* for a player's **node** and *node* for the
adversary's **botnet** (086 renamed them at r34, play unchanged, so no row).
