## The round (rulebook §4)

| | Step | Do |
|---|---|---|
| | Round start | Set your insight. Clear your used markers (below). |
| A | Sway | Each Fallout: 1 Discord. |
| B | Adversary | **Card** (threats; Escalate: each site of last round's type holding an agent takes 1 more; the board's rule; Infiltrate, Strike, Redeploy, other lines; each arrival checks only its own hex, once all its agents there are on), **Income** (next card up; each corrupted site's one output: a city 1 Discord, a station 1 research cube, a factory its agent pushed along its arrow then 1 new agent, a botnet 1 compute; then all compute onto the leftmost project at once; the research track advances a slot for every K cubes; firewalls, cordons and no-X tokens off). |
| C | Cards | Build a hand of 4, draft, keep (1 insight a tier-1; 3 a tier-2, 2 with the keep upgrade, less 1 per 2 Science tags you have). |
| D | Actions | All at once, until everyone passes. |
| E | Production | Read from the tracks, less each blocked chip's site: factories, cities (1 energy to every player with a node on or next to one), stations, megaprojects, income. |
| F | Threats | Each threat not paid off takes effect (and lands 1 agent on its first token), one that waited from last round included. |
| G | Cleanup | This round's effects end (Shelter, round flags). |

After every action and step: Unity just gained goes on its track; tip any full track, Discord first;
then check the losses, then the win (no city corrupted).

## Once a round (§5, §8, §20)

Each of these is used at most once a round. **Put a used marker on its used space when you use it** (each power, building slot and converter prints one), and clear them all at round start.

- **Your board's powers:** each once: each node power, one tier of each tiered power. Builder (Sprawl, Buy In, Annex, Scrap, Overcharge, Fortify); Protector (Outpost, Accord, Levy, Patrol, Interdict); Researcher (Peer Review, Field Survey, Field Station, Lab, Hack).
- **Each ambassador:** one move or one act; tip it over when it has.
- **Each building's Action** (`→`): once; "A, OR B" is one use.
- **Standard actions:** Remove a troop once a round, Turn a cube into insight once a round, each player.
- **Each megacorp's converter and chip converter:** once a round each, each player.
- **Vigil** (Protector): only the first 2 troops a round pay.
- **Aegis Doctrine** (Protector capstone): the first save a round.
- **A research slot's icon** happens once, as the marker arrives.

**Lasting effects, with their token:** a firewall, a cordon and a no-Infiltrate, no-Strike or no-Corrupt token come off in the next adversary turn's Income beat; Shelter ends at cleanup; a round-flag marker runs through the next round. Insight resets at round start (Patience keeps it); tipped ambassadors stand up.

## Tip strips (§12, §14)

Keep one tip-count marker a side on a strip like this, and read off its row the strength of the card drawn at that tip and the Discord cards left. You win the moment no city is corrupted, at any tip count.

| Tip count | Card drawn at this tip | Discord cards left |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | — | 7 |
| 1 | strength I | 6 |
| 2 | strength I | 5 |
| 3 | strength II | 4 |
| 4 | strength II | 3 |
| 5 | strength II | 2 |
| 6 | strength III | 1 |
| 7 | strength III | 0 |
| 8+ | strength III | **you lose** |

A track tips when it holds this much:

| Players | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unity | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 |
| Discord | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 |

## Corrupt numbers (§6, §21)

| Level | City | Station | Factory | Your node |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| 1-4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

Checked only when the adversary puts agents on a hex (an arrival), and only that hex: a site holding its number corrupts; else a hex with your node and that many loses the node. Each placement (a line, an Escalate, a factory's push, a threat's or card's agents) puts all its agents for a hex on, then checks that hex once. A Push or Gather is no arrival.

## Troops

Troops don't move or act on their own. They come late: from the research row, some projects and Discord cards.

1. **Garrison.** A troop stands where its line puts it; one that garrisons goes on its country's lowest-numbered corrupted factory; with none, its lowest-numbered corrupted city; with none, its lowest-numbered city (never the Protector's node hex: it stays in the supply). It stays there.
2. **Guard.** A corrupted site with a troop on or next to it can't be liberated. Remove the troop or push it away first.
3. **Strike** (an icon on the face-up event card, a round ahead, or a research slot): each troop, country by country in hex order, destroys one player node, or player's station or factory, on or next to it (the players choose; never anything on the Protector's node hex): destroyed unless protected (§13). The troop stays. Vigil: 1 insight for each troop on or next to the Protector's nodes.
4. **Redeploy** (an icon, rare): each troop is picked up and put on its country's corrupted city nearest a player node or ambassador (ties: the lower hex number); with none, it stays.

## Tracks and markers (§2)

- **Your income tracks** (your board): one marker a resource. Move it when a track space, program or building raises an income, or (your sites' output) when you gain or lose a station or factory or its megaproject completes; in production, collect what the markers show, less each blocked site's output (until its megaproject completes, a station's cube goes onto it).
- **Blocked chips:** on a producing site while an opposing piece stands there (an agent on yours or a neutral station, your ambassador on the adversary's) and on a city a blackout darkens; lift it when the last one leaves.
- **Your tag dials** (your board): one a tag. Turn it when a card with that tag comes into play or is sacrificed.
- **Used markers:** on each power, building slot and converter's used space; a bought chip on a city (above).
- **Unity and Discord tip tracks:** one space a tip; each space prints its card strength.
- **The adversary board:** the compute income track, the research row (six icon slots, a marker, and the cubes toward its next slot), the last-round space (the card whose sites escalate), a compute track beside each project slot (a marker, not cubes), and its corrupted sites' outputs.
- **Zone arrows** (the map): each industrial zone prints an arrow to its city; a corrupted factory there pushes its agent along it.
- **Waits token:** on a threat dealt after the counter phase or delayed; it takes effect next round.

## What spends an ambassador (§8)

- **An agent meets one** (it lands, or your ambassador arrives): one for one, both off.
- **Corruption** removes every ambassador on the hex.
- **Liberate** (your ready ambassador on the site acts): city 3, station 3, factory 3 (yours on the site pay first, then your supply).
- **Remove agents:** 3 from your supply.
- **Protecting** your node or facility on its hex (§13): one, instead of the piece.
- **Threats** that print ambassadors as a cost: from your supply.
- **Blocking** spends none: an ambassador on a corrupted site stops its output while it stands. A **move** is an ambassador's own action (a hex next to it, or any of your nodes); a troop never touches one.
- **Gain one:** 20 credits, Accord, cards.

## The adversary's outputs and research row

Each corrupted site makes one thing in the Income beat (a player ambassador on it stops it):

| Site | Output |
|---|---|
| City | 1 Discord |
| Research station | 1 research cube onto the adversary's track |
| Factory | its agent moves along its zone's arrow onto its city (not onto one already the adversary's); then, holding none, it makes 1 agent |
| Botnet | 1 compute |

**War Research** (every 3 cubes a country: one slot; its icon happens once, in each country):

1. **New factory**: Each country: a neutral factory opens on its lowest-numbered free industrial zone.
2. **Cells: factories**: Infiltrate: factories.
3. **Funding**: Income: +1 compute a country a round.
4. **Garrison**: Each country: 1 Troop garrisons its lowest-numbered corrupted factory (with none, its lowest-numbered corrupted city; with none, its lowest-numbered city).
5. **Strike**: Strike: each Troop destroys 1 player Node, research station or factory on or next to it.
6. **Garrison**: Each country: 1 Troop garrisons its lowest-numbered corrupted factory (with none, its lowest-numbered corrupted city; with none, its lowest-numbered city).
7. **The players lose.**

## Words on cards

- **Facility**: a research station or factory on the board. A building is always a card. Harden goes on a facility, or on your nodes where a card says so: one hardening token either way.
- **Ambassador**: a player's piece, any player's: a card never says whose.
- **Agent**: the adversary's piece on the board (the Security token is an Operative).
- **Troop**: an adversary troop.
- **In reach**: on a hex within 1 of your nodes.
- **Target**: a card, board power or standard action that touches the map names one target, a hex in your reach meeting its target box ("Target: a corrupted city"): it acts on it, or on or next to it, or in its country, as printed.
- **Gain**: into your supply.
- **Place**: onto the map: on the target, or next to it where printed.
- **A country you're in**: a country where you have a node.
- **Your home country**: the country you start in.
- **A threat**: on your card: one you may counter, near it (your node or ambassador on or next to one of its tokens).
- **Near it**: on a threat: on or next to one of its location tokens.
- **Here**: on a building: on this card, where its tokens sit.
- **Remove N**: as many as there are, up to N.
- **At N**: on a building's Effect: when its tokens here reach N, N of them come off and the rest happens.
- **Draw**: from the tier-1 deck, with no keep to pay; if it is empty, reshuffle its discard first.
- **On your cards**: on tags: on your cards in play, covered buildings included, and your board's tags, printed or revealed by its tracks. Tokens count only on the card that holds them.
- **Action**: once a round, always; "A, OR B" is one use, either half.
- **Open megaproject**: one that isn't complete; research cubes in your color go only onto one.
- **Liberate**: a corrupted site with no troop on or next to it: the agents on it come off and the adversary's cube comes off; it is left neutral, a city its country's for good, a station or factory for a card to claim (the action pays for it in ambassadors; a card's is unpaid).
- **Rescue**: on a card, a corrupted city: liberate it, then a neutral station or factory next to it with no agent on it becomes yours, unpaid and out of reach too; with none, nothing more happens. The Liberate action never claims.
- **Convert**: an agent on or next to the city leaves its stack and becomes your ambassador where it stood.
- **Gather up to N**: pieces (ambassadors, agents, troops; botnets never move) onto the target, each from a hex next to it; you may stop at any piece (a strike, "remove up to N on or next to it", moves nothing).
- **Push up to N**: pieces from the target, each to a hex next to it that you choose; you may stop at any piece.
- **Firewall**: a token on a hex: no agent lands or moves there (an Infiltrate's agent for it stays in the supply) and nothing corrupts or flips its site; it comes off at the end of the next adversary turn.
- **In the next adversary turn, no X**: a Unity card's prohibition: a token on each hex where the condition holds when the card resolves; that step skips a hex with its token (an Infiltrate's or Escalate's agent for it stays in the supply, and a factory pushes none onto it; no Strike: no troop strikes that hex; no Corrupt: agents may land, but nothing there corrupts, flips or loses a node), and the tokens come off with the firewalls.
