## The round (rulebook §4)

| | Step | Do |
|---|---|---|
| | Round start | Set your insight. Clear your used markers (below). |
| A | Sway | Each Fallout: 1 Discord. |
| B | Adversary | **Card** (threats, Infiltrate, Muster, other lines, Spill, Grow; each arrival checks only its own hex: a placement once all its agents there are on, a Spill's move as it lands), **March** (each stack 1 step), **Income** (next card up; compute step: each botnet seeds an agent next to it and adds its compute, the compute even when a cordon stops the seed or it has nowhere to land; then all compute onto the leftmost project at once; research track; firewalls, cordons and no-X tokens off). |
| C | Cards | Build a hand of 4, draft, keep (1 insight a tier-1; 3 a tier-2, 2 with the keep upgrade, less 1 per 2 Science tags you have). |
| D | Actions | All at once, until everyone passes. |
| E | Production | Read from the tracks, less each blocked chip's site: factories, cities (hosts), stations, megaprojects, income; then extra drones. |
| F | Threats | Each threat not paid off takes effect (and lands 1 agent on its first token), one that waited from last round included. |
| G | War | Each front fights. |
| H | Cleanup | This round's effects end (Shelter, round flags). |

After every action and step: Unity just gained goes on its track or against Discord; tip any full track, Discord first;
then check the losses, then the purge.

## Once a round (§5, §8, §20)

Each of these is used at most once a round. **Put a used marker on its used space when you use it** (each power, building slot and converter prints one; a city takes a bought chip), and clear them all at round start.

- **Your board's Growth and powers:** each once: one Growth option, one tier of each power. Builder (Growth, Scrap, Overcharge, Fortify); Protector (Growth, Patrol, Interdict); Researcher (Growth, Lab, Hack).
- **Each building's Action** (`→`): once; "A, OR B" is one use.
- **Standard actions:** Remove a troop once a round, Turn a cube into insight once a round, each player; **Buy energy** once a round a city, for all players together.
- **Each megacorp's converter and chip converter:** once a round each, each player.
- **Vigil** (Protector): only the first 2 troops a round pay.
- **Aegis Doctrine** (Protector capstone): the first save a round.
- **The adversary's research rule** fires at most once a round.

**Lasting effects, with their token:** a firewall, a cordon and a no-Infiltrate, no-Muster or no-Corrupt token come off in the next adversary turn's Income beat; Shelter ends at cleanup; a round-flag marker runs through the next round. Insight resets at round start (Patience keeps it); Dispatches refresh.

## Corrupt numbers (§6, §21)

| Level | City | Station | Factory | Your node |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 2 |
| 1-4 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 |

Checked only when the adversary puts agents on a hex (an arrival), and only that hex: a site holding its number corrupts; else a hex with your node and that many loses the node. Each placement (a line, a seed, a threat's or card's agents) puts all its agents for a hex on, then checks that hex once; a Spill moves one agent at a time, each move checked as it lands. A Push or Gather is no arrival.

## The march (§6)

The troops on each hex march as one stack, stack by stack (country by country, hex order), 1 step; troops on a hex a stack enters march later, on their own:

1. **Targets on or next to it?** (players' nodes and players' stations and factories; never anything on the Protector's node hex.) One troop strikes each, while troops last (the players choose): destroyed unless protected (§13). A troop that strikes leaves the map either way.
2. **The rest** move together 1 hex toward the nearest target by land, never onto the Protector's node hex; ties: the players choose, once for the stack. No way to any target: they stay.
3. Troops ignore ambassadors. Vigil: a troop ended its march (moved or not) next to the Protector's node.

## Tracks and markers (§2)

- **Your income tracks** (your board): one marker a resource. Move it when a track space, program or building raises an income, or (your sites' output) when you gain or lose a station or factory or its megaproject completes; in production, collect what the markers show, less each blocked site's output (until its megaproject completes, a station's cube goes onto it).
- **Blocked chips:** on a producing site while an opposing piece stands there (an agent on yours or a neutral station, your ambassador on the adversary's) and on a city a blackout darkens; lift it when the last one leaves.
- **Your tag dials** (your board): one a tag. Turn it when a card with that tag comes into play or is sacrificed.
- **Used markers:** on each power, building slot and converter's used space; a bought chip on a city (above).
- **Unity and Discord tip tracks:** one space a tip; each space prints its card strength, and Unity's the purge's.
- **The adversary board:** the compute income track, the research track, a compute track beside each project slot (a marker, not cubes), and its corrupted cities' and stations' output.
- **Waits token:** on a threat dealt after the counter phase or delayed; it takes effect next round.

## What spends an ambassador (§8)

- **An agent meets one** (it lands, or your ambassador arrives): one for one, both off.
- **Corruption** removes every ambassador on the hex.
- **Liberate:** city 5, station 4, factory 3 (yours on the site pay first, then your supply).
- **Remove agents:** 2 from your supply. **Envoy:** 1, for 1 Discord off.
- **A drone** landing on a base where one stands: one a drone.
- **Protecting** your node or facility on its hex (§13): one, instead of the piece.
- **Threats** that print ambassadors as a cost: from your supply.
- **Blocking** spends none: an ambassador on a corrupted site stops its output while it stands. A **Dispatch** moves one; a troop never touches one.
- **Gain one:** 16 credits, Accord, cards.

## Grow (§6)

**Grow: X** (the event card's last line), in each country:

1. **Which:** the X holding the most agents (tie: the lowest number) with a free hex next to it and fewer than 2 facilities next to it.
2. **What:** a neutral facility of the kind it has **fewer of next to it** (tie: a factory), else the other kind: a factory on a free industrial zone, a station on other open land (no facility, Fallout or base).
3. **Agent:** if that X holds an agent or is the adversary's, put 1 agent on the new facility (an arrival).

## Words on cards

- **Facility**: a research station or factory on the board. A building is always a card. Harden goes on a facility, or on your nodes where a card says so: one hardening token either way.
- **Ambassador**: a player's piece, any player's: a card never says whose.
- **Agent**: the adversary's piece on the board (the Security token is an Operative).
- **Troop**: an adversary troop; a megacorp's is a Drone, its own piece, waiting at its military bases.
- **In reach**: on a hex within 1 of your nodes.
- **Target**: a card, board power or standard action that touches the map names one target, a hex in your reach meeting its target box ("Target: a corrupted city"): it acts on it, or on or next to it, or in its country, as printed.
- **Gain**: into your supply.
- **Place**: onto the map: on the target, or next to it where printed.
- **A country you're in**: a country where you have a node.
- **Your home country**: the country you start in.
- **A threat**: on your card: one you may counter, near it (your node or ambassador on or next to one of its tokens).
- **Near it**: on a threat: on or next to one of its location tokens.
- **Here**: on a building: on this card, where its tokens sit.
- **Remove N**: as many as there are, up to N.
- **At N**: on a building's Effect: when its tokens here reach N, N of them come off and the rest happens.
- **Draw**: from the tier-1 deck, with no keep to pay; if it is empty, reshuffle its discard first.
- **On your cards**: on tags: on your cards in play, covered buildings included, and your board's tags, printed or revealed by its tracks. Tokens count only on the card that holds them.
- **Action**: once a round, always; "A, OR B" is one use, either half.
- **Open megaproject**: one that isn't complete; research cubes in your color go only onto one.
- **Liberate**: a corrupted site: the agents on it come off and the adversary's cube comes off; it is left neutral, a city its country's for good, a station or factory for a card to claim (the action pays for it in ambassadors; a card's is unpaid).
- **Rescue**: on a card, a corrupted city: liberate it, then a neutral station or factory next to it with no agent on it becomes yours, unpaid and out of reach too; with none, nothing more happens. The Liberate action never claims.
- **Convert**: an agent on or next to the city leaves its stack and becomes your ambassador where it stood.
- **Gather up to N**: pieces (ambassadors, agents, troops; botnets never move) onto the target, each from a hex next to it; you may stop at any piece (a strike, "remove up to N on or next to it", moves nothing).
- **Push up to N**: pieces from the target, each to a hex next to it that you choose; you may stop at any piece.
- **Firewall**: a token on a hex: no agent lands or moves there (an Infiltrate's agent for it stays in the supply) and nothing corrupts or flips its site; it comes off at the end of the next adversary turn.
- **In the next adversary turn, no X**: a Unity card's prohibition: a token on each hex where the condition holds when the card resolves; that step skips a hex with its token (an Infiltrate's agent for it stays in the supply; no Corrupt: agents may land, but nothing there corrupts, flips or loses a node), and the tokens come off with the firewalls.
